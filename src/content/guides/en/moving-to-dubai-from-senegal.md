This guide supports Senegalese people in Senegal and in the diaspora (France, Italy, Spain, the United States, Canada and elsewhere) who want to live and work in Dubai. It covers entering the country, paperwork, money, work and study. **Information checked on 27 November 2026**: visa rules change regularly, so always confirm them on the official sources cited at the bottom of the page.

## Dubai visa for Senegalese citizens: what you need to know

A Senegalese passport gives neither visa-free entry nor a visa on arrival in the UAE. The visa must therefore be **issued before departure**, and the passport must be valid for at least six months on the date of entry.

The official channels, according to the u.ae portal:

- the Emirati airline you are flying with (Emirates, Etihad, flydubai), under its conditions;
- a licensed hotel or travel agency, when you book through them;
- the online portals of the ICP or Dubai's GDRFA.

The UAE Embassy in Dakar does not issue tourist visas: no Emirati embassy does.

### Suspension rumours: how to find your way

Reports of a visa suspension for several African countries, including Senegal, circulated in 2022. The Senegalese Ministry of Foreign Affairs denied them at the time. This kind of rumour comes back regularly on social media: rely only on official sites (u.ae, icp.gov.ae, gdrfad.gov.ae) and on the embassy.

### The entry visas available

| Visa | Duration | Main conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Short tourist visa | Depends on the option offered | Requested by the airline, hotel, agency or online |
| 5-year multiple-entry tourist visa | 90 days per stay, extendable once by 90 days, 180 days a year at most | No sponsor, bank balance of USD 4,000 or equivalent over the last six months, health insurance valid in the UAE, return ticket |
| Job-seeker visa | 60, 90 or 120 days, single entry | Bachelor's degree at least, MOHRE skill level 1 to 3 or recent graduate of a top 500 university, financial guarantee |
| Visit to a resident relative | Depends on the visa granted | Requested by the resident who invites you (GDRFA rules) |

An overstay is charged AED 50 per day according to the ICP.

### Dual nationals and the Senegalese diaspora

With a French, Italian, Spanish, American or Canadian passport, you enter the UAE without a prior visa. Preferably use the same passport to enter, exit and build your residence file. A European or American residence permit does not give the right to a visa on arrival with a Senegalese passport: that scheme is reserved for a list of nationalities (extended on 25 June 2026 to Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines, Kenya and South Africa, according to the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs) which does not include Senegal.

## Living in Dubai: residence visas

Only a residence visa, together with the Emirates ID, lets you work and live in Dubai. You get it:

- as an **employee**, with the employer handling the work permit and the residence;
- as an **entrepreneur**, through your own company (trade, import-export, services): see our guide to [company formation in Dubai](/en/company-formation-dubai/);
- as a **freelancer**, in the free zones that offer it;
- through a resident **family member**;
- as a **student**, through the university;
- through the **Golden Visa**, for certain investors and talents.

The full process (entry permit, medical test, biometrics, Emirates ID) is in our guide to the [residence visa in Dubai](/en/residence-visa-dubai/).

## Legalising your documents in Senegal

The UAE has not joined the Hague Apostille Convention. A Senegalese document intended for the UAE must therefore go through a two-step legalisation chain, often completed by a translation.

| Step | Where | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Senegalese legalisation | Ministry of African Integration, Foreign Affairs and Senegalese Abroad, in Dakar | Present the originals. A power of attorney is first signed before a notary |
| 2. UAE attestation | UAE Embassy in Dakar (document attestation service) | The procedure (drop-off, timing, fees, possible use of the UAE ministry's digital service) is not published online: ask the embassy before travelling |
| 3. Translation | Licensed translator | Your documents are in French: a translation into Arabic or English is often required in the UAE |

Documents to prepare: diplomas and transcripts, marriage certificate, children's birth extracts, employment certificates. Allow several weeks of margin, especially if a start date or the beginning of the academic year is fixed.

**Contact details of the UAE Embassy in Dakar** (according to the UAE ministry): 230, route de la Corniche Ouest, Fann Mermoz, Dakar. Telephone +221 33 869 0000, email dakaremb@mofa.gov.ae, Monday to Friday from 8:30 to 15:30.

**Diaspora**: a diploma earned in France or Italy does not go through Dakar. It follows the legalisation chain of the country of issue, then the UAE Embassy in that country.

## Senegal's representation in the UAE

Senegal has an embassy in Abu Dhabi. The official list of foreign missions in the UAE shows no Senegalese consulate in Dubai, and the diaspora has asked for a consular office to be opened in the city. In practice, that means a trip to Abu Dhabi (about an hour and a half by road) for certain procedures. Two precautions:

- renew your passport in Senegal before leaving if it expires within the next two or three years;
- let the embassy know you are there as soon as you settle.

## Transferring your money: CFA franc, BCEAO and dirham

Senegal uses the CFA franc (XOF), issued by the BCEAO and pegged to the euro. The UAE dirham (AED) is pegged to the US dollar. Your conversions therefore depend mainly on the euro-dollar rate.

Financial relations with countries outside the West African Economic and Monetary Union (WAEMU) are governed by Regulation No. 06/2024/CM/UEMOA, adopted on 20 December 2024, and by the BCEAO's implementing instructions. In practice:

- a transfer to the UAE goes through an approved bank or intermediary, with supporting documents depending on the purpose;
- for a large amount (sale of a property, savings), first ask your bank for the list of documents required;
- keep proof of the origin of the funds: the UAE bank that opens your account will ask for it too;
- on arrival in the UAE, any cash amount above AED 60,000 (or the equivalent) must be declared through the ICP's Afseh system.

In the other direction, to send money to your family from Dubai, use a bank or an exchange house approved by the Central Bank of the UAE. It all starts with a local account: see our guide to [opening a bank account in Dubai](/en/open-bank-account-dubai/).

## The Senegal–UAE tax treaty

Senegal and the UAE signed in Dakar, on 22 October 2015, a treaty to avoid double taxation and prevent tax evasion on income taxes. A law of 5 April 2017 authorised its ratification. The UAE does not tax individuals' salaries. The treaty is shown as in force since 2017 in the OECD tables. If you keep income in Senegal, or if you remain a Senegalese tax resident, have your situation and the application of the treaty checked by a tax adviser and the Direction Générale des Impôts et des Domaines.

## Travelling to Dubai from Senegal: flights

- **Emirates** links Dakar (Blaise Diagne airport) and Dubai. The airline lists five flights a week, with variations by season.
- Other airlines offer the route with a stopover, for example via Addis Ababa, Casablanca or Istanbul.
- In 2026, the regional situation disrupted flights to the Gulf several times: choose a changeable ticket and check your schedule before departure.

## Working in Dubai as a Senegalese

Senegalese people work in Dubai in aviation, trade, hospitality, services or finance, and many Senegalese entrepreneurs use Dubai as a base for sourcing and export to West Africa. Labour rules are the same for all nationalities:

- no work without a permit issued by MOHRE to the employer;
- fixed-term, renewable contract;
- probation period of six months at most;
- **recruitment, travel, visa and residence costs are borne by the employer**: the law forbids making the employee pay them;
- keep a copy of the signed offer, and you can turn to MOHRE if the promised job is not provided.

### The most common scams

- an "agent" who asks for money for a work visa;
- an offer received on WhatsApp or Facebook with no verifiable company;
- a promise of a job on a simple tourist visa;
- a salary far above the market for the role.

Before accepting, check that the company exists, and never pay to get a job. For the job market and salaries, read our guide to [working in Dubai](/en/working-in-dubai/).

## Studying in Dubai after the Senegalese baccalaureate

The university that admits you sponsors your student visa. The route:

1. Admission to a recognised institution (most programmes are in English, a language test is often required).
2. Legalisation of the baccalaureate and transcripts in Dakar, then attestation by the UAE Embassy.
3. Certified translation into Arabic or English: the UAE Ministry of Education requires it for documents in another language.
4. Application for the equivalence of the secondary-school diploma, online through UAE Pass, with the admission letter.

More details in our guide to [studying in Dubai](/en/study-in-dubai/).

## Life on the ground

The Senegalese diaspora in the UAE is active, notably in trade with Senegal. English is the working language, Arabic the official language, and French opens the door to Dubai's francophone networks. The Senegalese driving licence cannot be exchanged directly: according to the RTA (page updated on 24 November 2026), 60 countries are on the exchange list and South Africa is the only African country. Plan lessons at a driving school if you want to drive.

## Checklist before leaving Senegal

1. Passport valid for several years.
2. Entry visa obtained through an official channel, never through an unlicensed intermediary.
3. Diplomas and certificates legalised at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, attested by the UAE Embassy in Dakar, translated.
4. Review with your bank of transfers outside WAEMU and the supporting documents.
5. Verified job offer or university admission.
6. Changeable ticket and health insurance for the first weeks.
7. Contact details of the Senegalese embassy in Abu Dhabi.
