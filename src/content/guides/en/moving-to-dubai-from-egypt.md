This guide is for Egyptians in Egypt and in the diaspora (the Gulf, Europe, North America) who are thinking about moving to Dubai to work, run a business or study. It covers relocating, not property investment. **Checked on 28 November 2026.** Visa, exchange-control and tax rules change, so we cite an official page for each rule and say plainly where we could not confirm a detail.

## Moving to Dubai from Egypt: the steps

| Step | Where | When |
|---|---|---|
| Choose the residence route: job, company, study, family, Golden Visa | UAE | 3 to 6 months before |
| Obtain the entry visa through an official channel | Airline, GDRFA or ICP | Before buying a non-refundable ticket |
| Attest degrees and civil-status documents | Egypt, then UAE embassy in Cairo | Before departure |
| Plan how you will move your money | Egypt | Before departure |

For the overview that applies to every nationality, see [moving to Dubai](/en/moving-to-dubai/).

## Dubai visa for Egyptians

The UAE government's visa page says GCC citizens need no visa, that citizens of several countries can get a visa on arrival, and that the list changes from time to time. Nationalities outside those groups must obtain a visa in advance. In the sources we checked, Egyptian passports were not in the exempt or visa-on-arrival groups, so plan for an advance visa. Confirm your case on the official entry-conditions tool before booking.

The channels listed by the portal for a visit visa are:

- an Emirati airline (Emirates, Etihad, flydubai) when you fly with it;
- a licensed hotel or travel agency in the UAE;
- the GDRFA Dubai portal or the Federal Authority (ICP) portal.

The passport must be valid for at least 6 months from the date of entry into the UAE. Many third-party sites quote fees and processing times that disagree with each other: read them on the channel you actually use. Be careful with offers from unlicensed "agents", and read our guide to [Dubai job scams](/en/blog/dubai-job-scams/) before paying anyone.

### Holders of a second passport or a foreign residence permit

If you hold a European, British, Canadian or US passport, entry follows the passport you travel on. A foreign residence permit does not by itself give an Egyptian passport the right to visa on arrival: the scheme is reserved for listed nationalities. Use the same passport on entry, exit and for your residence file.

## Residence in the UAE

A visit visa does not allow work. The routes to a residence visa and Emirates ID are:

- **Employer**: the company obtains the work permit and residence. Recruitment, visa and residence costs are the employer's responsibility: refuse any request to pay for them. See [working in Dubai](/en/working-in-dubai/).
- **Your own company**: [company formation in Dubai](/en/company-formation-dubai/) and [free zones](/en/free-zone-dubai/).
- **Freelance permit** in the free zones that offer it: [freelance visa](/en/freelance-visa-dubai/).
- **A resident relative**: [moving to Dubai with family](/en/moving-to-dubai-with-family/) and [sponsoring parents](/en/sponsor-parents-dubai-visa/).
- **University**: the university sponsors the student visa ([study in Dubai](/en/study-in-dubai/)).
- **Golden Visa** for certain investors and professionals: [Golden Visa guide](/en/golden-visa-dubai/).

The steps from entry permit to Emirates ID are in the [residence visa guide](/en/residence-visa-dubai/), and health insurance is required to obtain or renew the visa ([health insurance in Dubai](/en/blog/health-insurance-dubai/)).

## Attesting Egyptian documents for the UAE

Degrees, marriage certificates and children's birth certificates are the documents most often requested. According to the UAE embassy in Cairo's guidance, the order is:

1. **Egyptian Ministry of Foreign Affairs**: Egyptian-issued documents are attested first by the Egyptian ministry.
2. **UAE embassy in Cairo**: it attests the document through the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs' digital service. The original physical document is required.
3. **Delivery**: the embassy's guidance indicates delivery within 24 to 48 hours after the document is released by the embassy. Add the Egyptian ministry's own processing time.

Legal documents, such as a power of attorney, must be signed before a local Egyptian notary or competent authority, then attested by the Egyptian ministry.

Practical rules:

- keep documents in their original, unlaminated form;
- check the validity dates of stamps and signatures on the embassy page before submitting;
- attest the documents for the whole family in one batch;
- do not rely on an agent's claim that a single stamp is enough: follow the embassy's chain, which starts with the Egyptian foreign ministry.

Egyptians who hold documents issued in another country (for example a UK or Gulf degree) follow the chain of the issuing country, then the UAE embassy there.

## Moving your money from Egypt

Exchange rules for individuals are set by the Central Bank of Egypt and applied by your bank, and they have changed several times in recent years. We therefore publish no limit. What to do:

- ask your bank, in writing, which transfers abroad are allowed for your purpose and which documents it needs;
- use banks and licensed institutions only, and keep every receipt;
- keep proof of the origin of funds (payslips, tax records, sale contracts). UAE banks will ask for them too: see how to [open a bank account in Dubai](/en/open-bank-account-dubai/) and why banks sometimes refuse in [our guide on rejected accounts](/en/blog/uae-bank-account-rejected-reasons/);
- if you carry cash, follow the customs rules of both countries, and avoid travelling with large sums.

For a company, see the guide on a [business bank account for non-residents](/en/business-bank-account-dubai-non-resident/).

## Tax: Egypt, the UAE and the treaty

The UAE does not charge personal income tax on salaries: see [tax in Dubai](/en/tax-in-dubai/) for corporate tax and VAT. Whether Egypt still taxes you depends on your Egyptian tax residence and the type of income.

**The treaty.** The Egyptian Tax Authority lists an agreement between the governments of Egypt and the UAE for the avoidance of double taxation and the prevention of fiscal evasion on income taxes. The UAE Ministry of Finance reported that the two finance ministers signed a supplementary protocol on 11 February 2024. We did not find the date on which the protocol entered into force, so read the consolidated text before relying on a specific article.

**Proof of residence.** If you want to rely on the treaty, you will need evidence that you are a UAE tax resident. Our guide to the [UAE tax residency certificate](/en/uae-tax-residency-certificate/) explains the document and the conditions.

**What can remain taxable in Egypt.** Income arising in Egypt, such as rent from property you keep there, can remain taxable in Egypt. Ask the Egyptian Tax Authority or an Egyptian adviser to confirm your status. Men of military-service age should also check their situation with the Egyptian authorities before leaving.

## Planning the first three months

Most problems for newcomers come from timing, not from the rules themselves. A realistic order is: confirm the residence route and the visa channel first, start the Egyptian ministry and embassy attestation at the same time as the job or university paperwork, and book a flexible ticket only once the entry visa is issued. After arrival, the medical test, biometrics, Emirates ID and health insurance usually come in the first weeks, so keep copies of your passport, photos and attested documents in both paper and digital form. Allow a cash cushion for housing deposits and first rent, since landlords often ask for several payments upfront: our [cost of living in Dubai](/en/cost-of-living-dubai/) guide helps you size it. If you are moving with a spouse or children, prepare their attested marriage and birth certificates in the same batch, and read the sponsorship rules before you book school places.

## Checklist before leaving Egypt

1. Passport valid for several years, and at least 6 months on arrival.
2. Entry visa obtained through the airline, GDRFA or ICP.
3. Documents attested by the Egyptian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, then by the UAE embassy in Cairo, originals unlaminated.
4. Written answer from your bank on transfers and the paperwork required.
5. Egyptian tax residence confirmed, and treaty text checked if you plan to rely on it.
6. Verified job offer or admission letter, with no fee paid.
7. Health insurance valid in the UAE for the first weeks.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Paying for a visa or a job through an unlicensed intermediary.
- Arriving on a visit visa and starting work.
- Sending original documents to the embassy before the Egyptian ministry step is done.
- Moving a large sum without paperwork, which blocks your UAE bank account later.
