## Moving to Dubai from Pakistan: what is specific to Pakistani citizens

The UAE side (residence visa, medical test, Emirates ID, health insurance, bank account) is common to all nationalities and explained in our guide to [moving to Dubai](/en/moving-to-dubai/). For a Pakistani passport, four things need attention: the visa before departure, the attestation of your documents, your tax position and the movement of your money.

| Step | Body | When |
|---|---|---|
| Choose your residence route | Employer, company, investment or family | Months before |
| Attest your documents | Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Pakistan, then UAE Embassy | Before departure |
| Get your entry visa | Airline, hotel, agency or ICP / GDRFA platform | Before departure |
| Check your Pakistani tax position | Federal Board of Revenue | Before departure |
| Residence visa, Emirates ID, insurance | UAE authorities | First weeks |

## Dubai visa for Pakistani citizens

The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs lists Pakistan as **visa required**. Your visa is requested before you board, through an airline, a hotel, a licensed agency or the ICP and GDRFA platforms. Hold a passport valid for at least six months. A visit visa does not allow you to work: if you have a job offer, the employer files for the entry permit and residence. Visa conditions for specific nationalities do change, and agents' pages are often out of date, so check the UAE sources and be wary of anyone who sells a job offer. Our guide to [Dubai job scams](/en/blog/dubai-job-scams/) lists the usual traps.

## From visit to residence

The common routes are:

- **Employment.** The employer sponsors the visa; see [working in Dubai](/en/working-in-dubai/) and our page on the [Golden Visa for employees](/en/golden-visa-dubai-employees/) for skilled profiles.
- **A company you own.** A free zone or mainland licence lets the company sponsor its owner. The steps and costs are in our guide to [company formation in Dubai](/en/company-formation-dubai/).
- **Family.** A resident who earns at least 4,000 AED a month (or 3,000 AED with accommodation provided) can sponsor a spouse and children, according to u.ae.
- **Investment.** Certain categories exist; see the [Golden Visa guide](/en/golden-visa-dubai/). For property, [Cevitas Real Estate](https://www.cevitas.ae) is our partner agency.

The routes and conditions are compared in our guide to the [residence visa in Dubai](/en/residence-visa-dubai/). Health insurance is mandatory in Dubai for the visa.

## Getting Pakistani documents attested

Pakistan has applied the Apostille Convention since 9 March 2023, but the UAE does not appear in the Hague Conference status table (update of 30 June 2026). In practice, a Pakistani document follows two steps:

1. **Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Pakistan.** Pakistan-issued documents must be attested by the Ministry, except medical documents issued by GAMCA medical testing centres.
2. **UAE Embassy in Islamabad.** The embassy handles digital attestation: you log in to the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs website with UAE Pass, choose Pakistan as the country of issuance and pay online. A Ministry-approved courier collects the originals. Most requests are completed in a few business days, and you receive by email the document attested by both the embassy and the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Practical rules from the embassy: personal documents (birth, marriage, death certificates) must be submitted as originals, each document is handled separately because a bundle is not accepted, and laminated documents cannot be attested. Pakistani authorities sometimes suspend or change attestation services for a few days: check the Ministry's current notice before you submit. The documents you will usually need are degrees, a marriage certificate, children's birth certificates and, for certain jobs, a police certificate.

## Tax: Pakistan, the UAE and the 1993 convention

### What the UAE does

The UAE does not tax the personal income of individuals. Corporate tax applies to companies and to some individuals who run a business; see [tax in Dubai](/en/tax-in-dubai/). To prove that you live in the UAE for tax, the Federal Tax Authority issues a certificate, explained in our guide to the [UAE tax residency certificate](/en/uae-tax-residency-certificate/).

### What Pakistan does

Pakistan applies its own residence rules to decide whether you remain taxable there on your worldwide income. They depend on your days in Pakistan and on your ties. Income from Pakistani sources, such as rent from a house you keep, can remain taxable in Pakistan. We cannot settle your status in a general guide: ask the Federal Board of Revenue or a Pakistani adviser, and keep records of your days outside the country.

### The treaty

According to the FBR, the Pakistan-UAE income tax convention was signed on 7 February 1993 and entered into force on 30 November 1994. It has been modified by the Multilateral Instrument, which entered into force for the UAE on 1 September 2019 and for Pakistan on 1 April 2021 (FBR synthesised text). The convention covers income tax and, in the UAE, corporate tax. It includes rules to decide which country is the residence of a person claimed by both. It does not remove Pakistani tax on Pakistani-source income. Have a professional read it against your facts.

## Money: sending funds between Pakistan and the UAE

Money moves through banks and licensed exchange companies. The rules on the amounts you can send out of Pakistan, on foreign currency accounts and on bringing in remittances are set by the State Bank of Pakistan and change: check with your bank or the State Bank before sending large amounts, keep proof of the source of funds, and use official channels only. In the UAE, banks will ask for your Emirates ID, address and source of funds; see [opening a bank account in Dubai](/en/open-bank-account-dubai/) and [how much money you need to move to Dubai](/en/how-much-money-to-move-to-dubai/).

## Living in Dubai as a Pakistani: practical points

- **Consular services.** Pakistan has an embassy in Abu Dhabi and a Consulate General in Dubai (UAE list of foreign missions).
- **Passport and ID.** Renew your passport in good time: UAE residence requires a valid passport, and your residence file uses the same passport as your entry.
- **Cost of living.** Plan your budget with the [cost of living in Dubai](/en/cost-of-living-dubai/).
- **Family and schooling.** See [moving to Dubai with family](/en/moving-to-dubai-with-family/).
- **Driving.** Whether you can exchange your licence depends on the country that issued it, and the RTA keeps a list of 60 countries. Check the RTA list for your licence before you rely on it: our [driving licence guide](/en/blog/driving-licence-dubai/) explains the rules.

## Common mistakes when moving from Pakistan

- **Paying for a job offer.** A recruiter who charges you for a UAE job is a warning sign. The cost of recruitment and the visa is in principle borne by the employer.
- **Attesting in the wrong order.** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Pakistan comes first, then the UAE Embassy. Laminated documents and bundles are refused.
- **Forgetting the original.** The embassy requires the original of personal certificates, so keep the original safe and make several copies.
- **Treating the tax treaty as a shield.** The 1993 convention helps to avoid double taxation, but it does not decide your Pakistani residence, and Pakistani-source income such as rent can stay taxable in Pakistan.
- **Sending money through informal channels.** Unofficial transfers leave no proof of source, and a UAE bank will ask for that proof when you open an account or deposit a large sum.
- **Working on a visit visa.** It is not allowed and exposes you to fines and to a lack of protection if you have a dispute with an employer.

## Checklist before leaving Pakistan

1. Passport valid for at least six months and the entry visa obtained.
2. Residence route chosen: job, company, family or investment.
3. Documents attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, then by the UAE Embassy.
4. Pakistani tax position checked with the FBR or an adviser.
5. Money transferred through official channels, with proof of source.
6. Health insurance from arrival.
7. After arrival: residence visa, Emirates ID, bank account.
