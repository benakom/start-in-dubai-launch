It covers entry, papers, money, work and study. **Checked on 28 November 2026.** Visa rules change regularly: confirm them on the official sources listed at the bottom of the page.

| Step | Where | When |

## Dubai visa for Ivorians: what to know

The Ivorian passport gives neither visa-free entry nor visa on arrival in the UAE. The visa must therefore be **issued before departure**. For passport validity, follow the requirements of the airline and the visa you choose: allowing at least six months at the time of travel avoids unpleasant surprises.

According to the u.ae portal, the official channels are:

- the airline you fly with (Emirates, Etihad, flydubai), under its own conditions, including that you fly with it;
- a hotel or licensed travel agency in the UAE, when you book through them;
- the online portals of the ICP or Dubai's GDRFA.

UAE embassies, including the one in Abidjan, do not issue tourist visas.

### The entry visas available

| Visa | Duration | Main conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Short tourist visa | Depends on the option offered | Requested by the airline, hotel, agency or online |
| 5-year multiple-entry tourist visa | 90 days per stay, extendable by 90 days | Bank balance of USD 4,000 or equivalent over the last six months, health insurance valid in the UAE for 180 days, return or onward ticket |
| Visit to a resident relative | Depends on the visa granted | Requested by the resident who invites you (GDRFA rules) |

A tourist visa does not allow you to work. To look for a job or create a company, go through a residence visa or the options in our [residence visa guide](/en/residence-visa-dubai/) and the [job seeker visa](/en/job-seeker-visa-dubai/) guide.

### Ivorians in the diaspora and dual nationals

With a French, Belgian, Canadian or US passport, the entry rules are those of that passport, which for most of these nationalities does not require a prior visa: check the official page for your passport. Preferably use the same passport on entry, exit and for your residence file. The visa-on-arrival scheme extended on 25 June 2026 concerns Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines, Kenya and South Africa, for their citizens who hold a residence permit from certain countries. Ivory Coast is not on the list: a French or American residence permit therefore does not give you visa on arrival with an Ivorian passport.

## Living in Dubai: residence visas

Only a residence visa, with the Emirates ID, allows you to work and live in Dubai. You obtain it:

## The UAE embassy in Abidjan

According to the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the embassy in Abidjan opened in November 2022. It is at 1409 rue Gerty Archimede, Abidjan, open from 8:00 to 15:00 and closed on weekends and public holidays. Its contact address is AbidjanEMB@mofa.gov.ae.

Its site lists attestation and visa services for individuals without publishing the detail of procedures (documents, timing, fees). A ministry information line answers 24 hours a day on +971 800 44444. Do not travel without first obtaining the procedure by email or phone.

## Legalising your documents in Ivory Coast

Neither the UAE nor Ivory Coast appears in the table of parties to the Hague Apostille Convention (updated 30 June 2026). An Ivorian document intended for the UAE therefore does not receive an apostille: it goes through a legalisation chain, often completed by a translation.

| Step | Where | Advice |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Original document | Town hall, court or institution that issued it | Ask for a recent copy (birth extract, marriage certificate, diploma or pass certificate) |
| 2. Ivorian legalisation | Ivory Coast's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Abidjan | Present the originals. Ask about the competent office and the fees before travelling |
| 3. UAE attestation | UAE embassy in Abidjan (attestation service) | Ask the embassy for the procedure: it is not published in detail online |
| 4. Translation | Approved translator | Your documents are in French: a translation into Arabic or English is often required in the UAE |

Documents to prepare: degrees and transcripts, marriage certificate, children's birth extracts, employment certificates. Allow several weeks, especially if a start date or term date is set.

**Diaspora**: a degree earned in France or Canada does not go through Abidjan. It follows the legalisation chain of the issuing country, then the UAE embassy in that country.

## Ivory Coast's representation in the UAE

The official list of foreign missions in the UAE mentions an Ivorian embassy in Abu Dhabi (Al Muntazah area) and no consulate. To renew a passport or a civil-status document, plan a trip to Abu Dhabi, about an hour and a half by road from Dubai. Two precautions:

- renew your passport in Ivory Coast before leaving if it expires in the next two or three years;
- ask the embassy which procedures are possible from the UAE before you need them.

## Transferring money: CFA franc, BCEAO and dirham

Ivory Coast uses the CFA franc (XOF) of the West African Economic and Monetary Union, issued by the BCEAO and pegged to the euro. The UAE dirham (AED) is pegged to the US dollar. Your conversions therefore depend mostly on the euro-dollar rate.

Transfers to a country outside the WAEMU come under the regulation of the external financial relations of the Union's states, applied by banks and approved intermediaries. In practice:

- a transfer to the UAE goes through a bank or an approved intermediary, with supporting documents depending on the purpose (savings, sale of an asset, capital for a company);
- for a large amount, ask your bank first for the list of documents and the applicable limits;
- keep proof of the origin of funds: the UAE bank that opens your account will ask for it too;
- declare any cash you carry under the customs rules of Ivory Coast and the UAE, rather than travelling with large sums.

To send money to your family from Dubai, use a bank or exchange house licensed by the UAE Central Bank. Everything starts with a local account: see [how to open a bank account in Dubai](/en/open-bank-account-dubai/) and the guide on [business bank accounts for non-residents](/en/business-bank-account-dubai-non-resident/).

## The Ivory Coast-UAE tax treaty

A convention to avoid double taxation on income taxes links Ivory Coast and the UAE. According to an Ivorian firm quoting the text, it was ratified by decree no. 2023-437 of 24 May 2023 and caps withholding tax on dividends at 10%. An international firm lists it among the treaties in force between the UAE and WAEMU states, with entry into force in 2023.

These sources are not official: the text and its exact status are on the UAE Ministry of Finance treaties dashboard and in Ivory Coast's official gazette. The UAE does not tax personal salaries, but Ivorian tax can continue to apply if you remain a tax resident there or keep income there (rent, business activity). Have your case checked by a tax adviser and by the Direction generale des impots before invoking the treaty. Our [tax in Dubai](/en/tax-in-dubai/) guide explains the UAE framework, and the [UAE tax residency certificate](/en/uae-tax-residency-certificate/) guide covers proof of residence.

## Flying to Dubai from Ivory Coast

- **Emirates** flies Abidjan-Dubai. On its Ivorian site the airline states 7 flights a week, varying by season. The page does not say whether the flight is direct: check on your ticket.
- Other airlines link the two cities with a stop.
- Choose a flexible ticket and check your schedule before departure: the regional situation disrupted some Gulf flights in 2026.

## Working in Dubai as an Ivorian

The labour rules are the same for every nationality:

- no work without a permit issued by MOHRE to the employer;
- **recruitment, travel, visa and residence costs are the employer's**: you must not be made to pay them;
- keep a copy of the signed offer, and you may appeal to MOHRE if the promised job is not provided.

Many Ivorian entrepreneurs use Dubai as a base for sourcing and exporting to West Africa. A free-zone or mainland company can suit this, depending on the activity: see the guides to [free zones](/en/free-zone-dubai/) and [how much money you need to move](/en/how-much-money-to-move-to-dubai/).

### The most common scams

- an "agent" who asks for money for a work visa;
- an offer received by WhatsApp or Facebook with no verifiable company;
- a promise of work on a simple tourist visa;
- a salary far above the market for the position.

Before accepting, check that the company exists, and never pay to get a job. See [Dubai job scams](/en/blog/dubai-job-scams/).

## Studying in Dubai after the Ivorian baccalaureate

The university that admits you sponsors your student visa. The path:

1. Admission to a recognised institution (most courses are in English, a language test is often requested).
2. Legalisation of the baccalaureate and transcripts in Ivory Coast, then attestation by the UAE embassy.
3. Certified translation into Arabic or English for documents in another language.
4. Request for an equivalence of the secondary-school leaving certificate from the UAE Ministry of Education, with the admission letter.
