## Moving to Dubai from Cameroon: what is specific to Cameroonians

The UAE steps (residence visa, medical test, Emirates ID, health insurance, bank account) are the same for every nationality; our guide to [moving to Dubai](/en/moving-to-dubai/) lists them. This page covers what is particular to a Cameroonian passport: the visa before departure, the consulate in Dubai, the legalisation of your documents, transfers within the CEMAC zone and the tax questions you should ask.

## Dubai visa for Cameroonian citizens

The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs lists the Republic of Cameroon as **visa required**. You do not get a visa on arrival: that scheme only covers nationalities holding a residence permit from approved countries, and Cameroon is not among those named in the June 2026 release. The visa is applied for online through the ICP or Dubai's GDRFA, or through an airline, a hotel or a licensed agency. Check the current conditions on the official platforms, because requirements for some nationalities change and agency pages are often out of date. Hold a passport valid for at least six months.

A visit visa does not allow you to work. For a job offer, the employer sponsors your entry and residence; read [working in Dubai](/en/working-in-dubai/). Also read our guide to [Dubai job scams](/en/blog/dubai-job-scams/): a recruiter who asks you to pay for a visa or a job is a warning sign.

## From visit to residence

The common routes are:

- **Employer.** The company issues the entry permit and residence visa; see the [residence visa in Dubai](/en/residence-visa-dubai/). If you are already in the UAE on a visit visa while you look for work, the [job seeker visa](/en/job-seeker-visa-dubai/) explains an option.
- **University.** A student visa through an accredited institution: see [study in Dubai from Africa](/en/study-in-dubai-from-africa/) and the [student visa in Dubai](/en/student-visa-dubai/).
- **Your own company.** A free zone or mainland company can sponsor its owner: [company formation in Dubai](/en/company-formation-dubai/).
- **Family.** A resident who meets the income condition (at least 4,000 AED a month, or 3,000 AED with accommodation provided, according to u.ae) can sponsor a spouse and children.

Health insurance is mandatory in Dubai for the visa.

## Cameroon's representation in the UAE

According to the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Cameroon has a **Consulate in Dubai** headed by a Consul General. The Ministry's page does not display its address or telephone: its toll-free line (80044444) can help, and the Cameroonian diplomatic service is the authority for current contacts. Use the consulate for passport renewal, consular certificates and questions on Cameroonian civil-status documents, and register with it when you arrive.

## Legalising Cameroonian documents

You will need documents for several procedures: degrees and transcripts for a work visa or a university, a marriage certificate to sponsor a spouse, children's birth certificates, and sometimes a police certificate.

- **No apostille shortcut.** Neither Cameroon nor the UAE appears in the Hague Conference Apostille status table (update of 30 June 2026). A document therefore goes through certification and legalisation instead of a single apostille.
- **The typical chain.** The document is certified by the competent Cameroonian authority (a court, a ministry or a notary depending on the document), then legalised by the Cameroonian Ministry in charge of external relations, then by the UAE diplomatic mission that covers Cameroon, then attested by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs once in the UAE if the receiving body asks for it. This chain is described from general practice: the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs does not publish a Cameroon-specific procedure we could verify, so confirm each step with the mission before you submit.
- **Translation.** Documents in French usually need an Arabic or English translation by a sworn translator when the UAE body asks for it.
- **Keep copies and originals.** Personal certificates are usually required in original form.

## Money: CEMAC transfers and your accounts

Cameroon belongs to the CEMAC zone, where the central bank BEAC and the banking commission supervise banks and the exchange regulation sets the rules for transfers abroad. In practice, transfers go through an approved bank with supporting documents (a contract, a tuition invoice, a proof of origin of funds). We have not verified current limits: ask your bank in writing and keep every proof.

Three habits protect you: use approved channels only, keep the origin of funds documented because a UAE bank will also ask for it, and compare fees and exchange rates before sending. The UAE side is explained in [opening a bank account in Dubai](/en/open-bank-account-dubai/) and [how much money you need to move to Dubai](/en/how-much-money-to-move-to-dubai/).

## Tax: what to check

- **UAE.** The UAE does not tax the personal income of individuals; see [tax in Dubai](/en/tax-in-dubai/) for corporate tax and VAT. The Federal Tax Authority can issue a residence certificate: see the [UAE tax residency certificate](/en/uae-tax-residency-certificate/).
- **Cameroon.** Whether you remain taxable in Cameroon depends on the Cameroonian tax code and on your ties (home, family, income). Ask the tax administration or an adviser, and keep proof of the dates you leave.
- **Treaty.** We could not confirm a tax treaty between Cameroon and the UAE. Check the UAE Ministry of Finance dashboard and the Cameroonian administration's list before assuming that one applies.

## Common mistakes when moving from Cameroon

- **Paying an agent for a visa or a job.** A request for money in return for a UAE job is a warning sign. Verify the employer directly and keep your contract.
- **Assuming an apostille is enough.** Neither country is in the Hague status table: ask the competent authorities for the full chain before you pay for any service.
- **Sending a large sum without a paper trail.** Your bank, and later a UAE bank, will want the origin of funds and the purpose of the transfer.
- **Arriving without a plan for the first weeks.** A visit visa has a limited duration. Fix your residence route (job offer, university, company or family sponsor) before the stay runs out.
- **Skipping the consulate.** Register with the Consulate of Cameroon in Dubai: it is your point of contact for passport and civil-status matters.
- **Assuming a treaty exists.** No treaty could be confirmed on the lists consulted: have your tax position checked before you change your residence.

## Checklist before leaving Cameroon

1. Passport valid for at least six months and the entry visa obtained.
2. Residence route chosen: employer, university, company or family.
3. Documents certified and legalised, with translations where needed.
4. Consulate of Cameroon in Dubai contacted for registration.
5. Transfers prepared through an approved bank, with proof of the origin of funds.
6. No payment made to a recruiter or agent for a promised job.
7. Health insurance from arrival.
8. After arrival: residence visa, Emirates ID, bank account.
