## The honest answer: scholarships exist, but they are selective

Searches for "scholarships for international students in the UAE" return long lists with titles like "fully funded" and "apply now". Many of these lists are copied from one another, and some link to awards that are closed, restricted to UAE nationals, or not scholarships at all.

So this page does something different. It reports **only what universities and the UAE government publish on their own sites**, with the date we consulted them (November 2026), and it says what we could not check. Where an award is only for citizens, we leave it out.

Two points before the details:

- **Most awards go to the strongest applicants.** The thresholds the universities publish (for example 95% or more in Grade 12) show how selective they are.
- **No page we read guarantees an award.** Each says that meeting the criteria does not mean you will get one.

If your budget depends on a scholarship you do not yet have, plan an alternative. Our guide to the [cheapest universities in Dubai](/en/cheapest-universities-dubai/) shows how to compare fee grids, and [study in Dubai](/en/study-in-dubai/) explains the types of institutions.

## What the universities publish

### Khalifa University (Abu Dhabi)

**Undergraduate.** The scholarships page lists a category for **international students in five tiers**:

| Tier | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Tier I | full tuition fees, plus textbooks |
| Tier II | 75% of tuition fees, plus textbooks |
| Tier III | 50% of tuition fees, plus textbooks |
| Tier IV | 25% of tuition fees, plus textbooks |
| Tier V | textbooks only |

The university says that applicants **do not submit a separate scholarship application**: eligibility is assessed during admission, international students are placed in a tier based on academic merit and other relevant factors, the number of slots is limited, and meeting the minimum criteria does not guarantee an award. The page applies to full-time Bachelor's programmes and does not state a GPA cut-off.

**Graduate.** For master's students, the page lists full or partial tuition coverage, textbooks, accommodation, medical insurance, and visa and travel support. PhD students receive that package plus a monthly stipend and other support subject to funding. Awards are merit-based and you indicate your interest when you submit the admission application. A separate scholarship for Chinese nationals exists, and a programme for UAE nationals: they are not open to everyone.

### United Arab Emirates University, UAEU (Al Ain)

According to UAEU's page, a tuition discount covers **international students** (enrolled from inside or outside the UAE) and children of staff, **from the 2025/2026 academic year**. It covers **tuition fees only**: housing and the admission application fee are not covered. The tiers include:

- **Chancellor's Scholarship for Elite Students, 100%:** 98% in Grade 12, English and mathematics test scores, an Arabic test for Law, and a continuing GPA of 3.8.
- **Elite Students, 75%:** 95% in Grade 12, lower test thresholds, continuing GPA of 3.6.
- **Talented Students, 100% or 50%:** evidence of talent in innovation, arts, sports or technology, plus any tests or interviews.

The rules also say that only one scholarship can be held at a time, repeated courses are not covered, and awards depend on available seats. New students must apply within one academic year of finishing high school.

### American University of Sharjah, AUS (Sharjah)

AUS lists a wider range of undergraduate awards. Those relevant to international applicants include:

- **President's Scholarship:** 100% tuition and lab fees, application fee waiver, monthly stipend, on-campus housing, three round-trip tickets and medical insurance, for students who **completed secondary education outside the UAE**, with portfolio and video required.
- **Chancellor's Scholarship:** full tuition and lab fees, housing and insurance, for students who completed secondary education **in the UAE**.
- **Merit Scholarship:** 10 to 20% of tuition and lab fees, offered automatically at admission to students with a Grades 10 and 11 average of 95% or above.
- **Financial Grant:** based on financial need, for first-time students with an admission letter and limited resources.

AUS also lists graduate assistantships (tuition waivers and stipends). The page says grants are contingent on available funding. To apply, you first receive an admission offer, then submit the scholarship application through the links on the page.

### Other universities

We tried to read the scholarship pages of other well-known institutions (for example MBZUAI and NYU Abu Dhabi), but the pages were not accessible to us when we wrote this guide, so we make no claim about them. Check each university's own website, and go straight to the admissions or financial aid office. Private universities in Dubai may also advertise merit awards: for example one fee page we cited in our cheapest-universities guide mentions merit scholarships of up to 50%, which are not included in the listed fees.

## The Golden Visa for outstanding students

This is not a scholarship, but it matters for your plans. u.ae lists a Golden Visa category for **outstanding students**: a **5-year** visa for high school achievers and a **10-year** visa for top university students. The documents required are certificates of excellence and recommendation letters from the school or university. The page does not give the grade cut-offs, so check the ICP website. See our guide to the [Golden Visa for students](/en/golden-visa-dubai-students/) and the general [Golden Visa](/en/golden-visa-dubai/).

## You still need a student visa

A scholarship pays fees. It does not give you the right to live in the UAE. u.ae explains that a student can be sponsored by a parent who is a UAE resident, or by the accredited university or college, and that the student submits a certificate from the institution stating the duration of study. Read our guide to the [student visa in Dubai](/en/student-visa-dubai/). If you plan to work part-time, read [student work rules in Dubai](/en/student-work-rules-dubai/) first.

## How to apply: a practical plan

1. **Shortlist universities** whose published fee grid and scholarship page you have read.
2. **Check the deadline** and whether the scholarship is requested with the application or after the offer. Some, like AUS's President's Scholarship, need a portfolio or video.
3. **Prepare your documents**: certified school results, certificates, recommendation letters, English test scores, passport copy. See our guide for [students from Africa](/en/study-in-dubai-from-africa/) for diploma recognition.
4. **Apply on the official portal only.** Type the address yourself or follow it from the university's own site.
5. **Keep a fallback.** Prepare a budget that works without the scholarship, or with a partial one.
6. **Read the scholarship contract** before you sign: GPA, credit loads, duration, what is covered and what is not.

## Scams to avoid

- **Anyone who asks for a fee to "release" a scholarship.** Universities in the pages we read assess scholarships through their own admissions process.
- **Messages claiming you "won" a scholarship you never applied for.**
- **Lookalike websites.** Check the address letter by letter.
- **Agents who promise a guaranteed award.** No page we read guarantees one.

If in doubt, write to the university's admissions office from the address on its official website. Our article on [Dubai job scams](/en/blog/dubai-job-scams/) shows the same patterns applied to job offers.
