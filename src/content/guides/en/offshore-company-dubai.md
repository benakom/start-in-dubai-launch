## What is an offshore company in Dubai?

The word "offshore" is both appealing and worrying. In the UAE, it has a precise meaning: a company registered with an offshore registry, which **is not allowed to carry out commercial activity with persons located in the UAE**. It receives a certificate of incorporation, not a trade licence.

The two registries most used by entrepreneurs targeting Dubai are:

- **JAFZA Offshore**, attached to the Jebel Ali Free Zone in Dubai;
- **RAK ICC** (Ras Al Khaimah International Corporate Centre), in the emirate of Ras Al Khaimah.

In both cases, you must go through an approved **registered agent**: RAK ICC does not accept direct applications from individuals, and JAFZA requires an agent and a registered office in Dubai.

## Offshore company or free zone company: the real difference

| Criterion | Offshore company | Free zone company |
|---|---|---|
| Document obtained | Certificate of incorporation | Trade licence |
| Activity in the UAE | Prohibited | Allowed within the zone, restricted on the mainland |
| International activity | Yes | Yes |
| Office | None, registered office with the agent | Flexi-desk, coworking or office |
| Residence visas | None | Yes, depending on the quota |
| Employees in the UAE | No | Yes |
| Bank account in the UAE | Possible but difficult | More accessible |
| Main use | Holding, structuring | Real activity |

If your goal is to live and work in Dubai, the [free zone in Dubai](/en/free-zone-dubai/) or the mainland are the right options. The guide to [company formation in Dubai](/en/company-formation-dubai/) compares the three structures in detail.

## What an offshore company in the UAE is used for

Used honestly and with the right advice, an offshore company can be used to:

- **hold shareholdings** in other companies, in the UAE or elsewhere (holding);
- **hold assets** such as trademarks, patents or copyrights;
- **carry an activity aimed abroad**, for example international trade or consulting for customers outside the UAE;
- **hold a property in Dubai** in some cases: JAFZA cites this use, subject to JAFZA's approval and Dubai Land Department rules. For the real estate side itself, speak to [Cevitas Real Estate](https://www.cevitas.ae), a RERA-licensed agency.

## What it is not for

Let us be clear, because many online offers feed the confusion:

- **No visa.** An offshore company does not let you reside in the UAE or sponsor your family.
- **No local activity.** You cannot invoice customers located in the UAE, rent an office or hire on the spot.
- **No anonymity.** The UAE requires companies to keep a register of their ultimate beneficial owners (Cabinet Resolution No. 109 of 2023), and banks always identify the people who control the company.
- **No automatic exemption in your country.** If you remain tax resident in France, Belgium, Morocco or elsewhere, your country may tax the company's profits, in particular if it considers that the company is managed from your home.

## Opening a bank account with an offshore company

UAE banks apply the same vigilance to all customers, and they are more cautious with structures that have no local activity. Expect to provide:

- the certificate of incorporation, the articles of association and the shareholder register;
- the identity and proof of address of all beneficial owners and directors;
- a precise description of the activity, with identified contracts, invoices or customers;
- the origin of funds and wealth;
- sometimes, proof of a link with the UAE (resident partner, related activity).

Refusal is frequent when the file is vague. Our guide to [opening a bank account in Dubai](/en/open-bank-account-dubai/) details what banks look at.

## Economic substance and corporate tax

### The economic substance rules

Between 2019 and 2022, the UAE required "economic substance" reports from certain companies. The Ministry of Finance announced in 2024 that these rules no longer apply to financial years after 2022, in connection with the introduction of corporate tax. Obligations for the 2019 to 2022 years remain due.

The notion of substance has not disappeared, however. It still matters for:

- corporate tax in the UAE (for example for the free zone regime);
- banks, which want to see a real activity;
- the tax authority of your country, which can challenge a company with no real means.

### Corporate tax

A company registered in the UAE, offshore included, is in principle a resident person for corporate tax purposes: Article 11 of Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 covers any legal person established or recognised under the legislation applicable in the UAE. It must register with the Federal Tax Authority, and for a company set up since 1 March 2024, the deadline is 3 months after creation, under penalty of a 10,000 AED fine. The rate is 0% up to 375,000 AED of taxable profit and 9% above, except for exemptions provided by law (certain dividends and capital gains on shareholdings, for example). For the full picture, read our guide to [taxes in Dubai](/en/tax-in-dubai/).

## Offshore accounts and tax transparency (CRS)

The era of secret "offshore accounts" is over, Dubai included.

- **Automatic exchange of information:** the UAE applies the OECD Common Reporting Standard (CRS). According to the Ministry of Finance, the scheme has been in force in the UAE since 1 January 2017. Banks report every year the accounts held by non-residents, and this information is passed to the account holder's country of tax residence.
- **CRS 2.0:** the Ministry of Finance announced at the end of 2025 that the updated version of the standard would apply from 1 January 2027, with a first exchange in 2028.
- **Companies are affected too:** for an account opened in the name of a company with no real activity, the bank identifies the people who control it, and they are the ones reported.
- **Your country has its own obligations:** in France, for example, accounts opened, held or closed abroad must be declared every year with the income tax return (form 3916, according to impots.gouv.fr). In Morocco, residents remain subject to the Office des Changes foreign exchange rules for their assets and investments abroad: check on oc.gov.ma what is permitted and what must be declared or authorised. Ask your country's authority.

## Warnings to keep in mind

- **Be wary of "zero tax, zero reporting" promises.** They are false for a tax resident of a country that taxes its residents on worldwide income.
- **Never use a nominee.** The beneficial owner register and bank checks make this arrangement dangerous, and illegal in many countries.
- **Do not confuse offshore with relocation.** An offshore company does not make you a UAE resident. For that, see our guide to [moving to Dubai](/en/moving-to-dubai/).
- **Check the registered agent.** It must be approved by the registry (JAFZA or RAK ICC). Ask for its approval and a written contract.
- **Plan for the bank before creating the company.** An offshore company without a bank account is useless.
- **Keep clean accounts.** Even with no activity in the UAE, the company must be able to justify its flows, contracts and accounts, to the bank as well as to the tax authority.

## JAFZA Offshore or RAK ICC: how to choose

The two registries are similar on the essentials (no activity in the UAE, no visa, registered agent mandatory). The differences lie in practical points:

- **the intended use**, in particular if the company is to hold a property in Dubai, which JAFZA highlights;
- **the fees** for creation and renewal, set by the registry and by the agent;
- **the documents required** and processing times;
- **how your bank perceives it**, as it may prefer one or the other depending on your profile.

Compare written quotes from two approved agents before deciding.

## Before creating an offshore company: the checklist

1. Write in one sentence what the company will hold or do, and for which customers.
2. Check that no activity will target customers located in the UAE.
3. Confirm that you do not need a visa through this company.
4. Consult a tax adviser in your country of residence on the taxation of profits.
5. Choose the registry (JAFZA Offshore or RAK ICC) and an approved registered agent.
6. Prepare the bank file: activity, contracts, origin of funds.
7. Note the deadline for corporate tax registration in the UAE.
