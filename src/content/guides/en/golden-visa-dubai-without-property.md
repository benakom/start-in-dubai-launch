## Do you need to buy property to get the Golden Visa?

No. Buying property of at least 2 million AED is the best-known route, but it is only one of several. The ICP and the GDRFA describe other categories that rest on a financial investment, a company, a job, a talent or a record of achievement.

If you want the property route, the real estate side belongs to our partner [Cevitas Real Estate](https://www.cevitas.ae), a RERA-licensed agency; this page does not cover it. For all categories and the shared benefits, read our main guide to the [Golden Visa in Dubai](/en/golden-visa-dubai/).

The routes below are grouped by what you bring: capital, a company, a job or a profile.

## Route group 1: investing capital, without property

### A share in a company

According to the GDRFA investor page, you can qualify by holding a **share in a UAE company worth at least 2 million AED**, supported by a certified financial report, a trade licence, a bank statement and tax documents. The authority also assesses the size of the investment, the employment it creates, administrative efficiency and financial solvency. The ICP words it as a company with capital of at least 2 million AED, or a partner's share of at least that amount.

### A bank deposit

The GDRFA investor page lists a deposit of **at least 2 million AED** in a local UAE bank, or in local sukuk or investment bonds. Conditions to know:

- the amount must be **frozen for at least two years**;
- withdrawal is **not permitted during the 10-year period**;
- you must **prove housing in Dubai**.

The ICP also mentions a deposit in an investment fund or a national bank. Because the pages do not read exactly the same, ask the issuing authority which form of deposit it accepts and how long the funds must remain.

### A company paying taxes

Both the GDRFA and the ICP mention a company that pays **at least 250,000 AED in taxes a year**. The GDRFA specifies proof through a letter from the Federal Tax Authority plus a trade licence with a partners' appendix. This route suits an established business, not a start-up.

### What applies to all investor routes

- The funds must be **yours**, and the origin must be justified. Our guide to [investing in Dubai](/en/invest-in-dubai/) explains the types of investment.
- You must be able to **support yourself and your family** without government assistance.
- The authority may **check that you still meet the conditions** during the validity of the permit.
- The GDRFA states that a financial deposit cannot be refunded during the 10-year period.
- Permit holders are exempt from the 180-day absence rule.

## Route group 2: entrepreneurs

If you run or found a company, you may qualify without placing a deposit. According to the ICP, one of these situations applies:

- a UAE-registered SME in an approved field with at least **1,000,000 AED of annual revenue**;
- a business idea approved by an accredited incubator, the Ministry of Economy or a competent local authority;
- one or more ventures that were sold for at least **7,000,000 AED** in total.

In Dubai, the GDRFA also requires a nomination. Details, fees and a table of routes are in our page on the [Golden Visa for entrepreneurs](/en/golden-visa-dubai-entrepreneurs/). To set up a company first, read [company formation in Dubai](/en/company-formation-dubai/).

## Route group 3: employees

A skilled employee can obtain the Golden Visa without investing if they hold a MOHRE level 1 or 2 position, a valid UAE employment contract and at least a bachelor's degree, and earn at least **30,000 AED a month**. Details, documents and the question of what counts as salary are in our page on the [Golden Visa for professionals and employees](/en/golden-visa-dubai-employees/). If you earn less, the Green Visa for skilled workers (15,000 AED, MOHRE levels 1 to 3, 5 years, according to the ICP) is another option to compare in our guide to the [residence visa in Dubai](/en/residence-visa-dubai/).

## Route group 4: talents, students and humanitarian profiles

These categories have no financial threshold, but they require evidence of a record and, often, a recommendation.

- **Exceptional talents and rare specialisations.** The ICP covers doctors, scientists, inventors, PhD holders, specialists in priority fields, creatives and top athletes. You need a recommendation from the competent body and evidence of your track record. The u.ae page gives the duration as 10 years for this group.
- **Outstanding students.** The u.ae page lists 5 years for high school achievers and 10 years for top university students, with certificates of excellence or recommendation letters. Our main guide gives the ICP's grade conditions.
- **Humanitarian pioneers and frontline professionals.** The u.ae page gives 10 years, with certificates of appreciation, documentation of the humanitarian contribution or at least five years of service.

## Comparison of routes without property

| Route | Main condition | Evidence | Authority to check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company share | At least 2 million AED stake | Financial report, licence, bank statement, tax documents | GDRFA or ICP |
| Bank deposit | At least 2 million AED, frozen at least two years | Bank certificate, proof of housing | GDRFA or ICP |
| Company paying taxes | At least 250,000 AED taxes a year | Federal Tax Authority letter, licence | GDRFA or ICP |
| Entrepreneur | 1,000,000 AED revenue, incubator approval or 7,000,000 AED exit | Accounts, incubator letter or sale documents, nomination in Dubai | GDRFA or ICP |

| Talent | Recommendation and track record | Awards, publications, letters | Competent body, then ICP or GDRFA |
| Student | Exceptional results | Certificates, recommendation letters | ICP or GDRFA |

## What if none of these routes fits you?

The Golden Visa is not the only long-term residence. Depending on your situation, a company visa, a freelance permit, the Green Visa or a standard employment visa may be enough, often at lower cost and with less proof. Our [residence visa guide](/en/residence-visa-dubai/) compares them, and our [freelance visa guide](/en/freelance-visa-dubai/) covers independent workers.

Before you choose, keep in mind that a long-term visa does not decide your tax residence. Read [taxes in Dubai](/en/tax-in-dubai/).

## How to compare the routes honestly

Three questions help to cut through the noise.

**Do you have capital or a profile?** Investor routes need at least 2 million AED of your own money and a long-term commitment: the GDRFA says a deposit cannot be withdrawn during the 10-year period. Professional and entrepreneur routes need a job or a company that already meets the thresholds.

**How long will you stay?** The Golden Visa lets you stay outside the country for long periods without losing residence, according to the GDRFA, but your home country may still treat you as a tax resident. A route that ties up capital for ten years makes sense only if you plan to use the residence for that long.

**What does the authority actually ask for?** Official pages for the same category sometimes differ (for example on duration, 5 or 10 years, in the case of entrepreneurs). Ask the authority that will issue your visa, and keep the answer. In Dubai, the GDRFA offers service pages and Customer Happiness Centres for this purpose.

Finally, remember that the cheapest option on paper can be the most expensive if the file is refused after you have paid for translation, attestation and insurance. Check eligibility first, then pay.

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming that a deposit can be withdrawn after the first two years.** The GDRFA states it cannot be withdrawn during the 10-year period.
- **Mixing up conditions from different categories.** A salary threshold applies to employees, not to investors.
- **Using borrowed money.** Funds must be yours and their origin must be justified.
- **Trusting a promise of guaranteed approval.** Only the authority can decide.
- **Forgetting health insurance and housing proofs**, which several routes require.

## Checklist before you decide

1. Write down your profile: capital, company, job or record.
2. Identify the matching route in the table above and read its official page.
3. Check each threshold with up-to-date documents.
4. Ask the ICP or the GDRFA for the duration and the fees for your category.
5. Plan insurance, housing proof and document attestation.
6. Consider tax residence in your home country before you move.
