This guide is for Algerians living in Algeria and for those in the diaspora (France, Canada, Belgium and elsewhere) who are considering Dubai. It covers relocating, not investing. The context between the two countries has shifted a lot in 2026. **Situation as of 27 November 2026, subject to change**: rules can change from one week to the next, so always confirm with the consular authorities and on official sites before buying a ticket or resigning.

## Algeria and the UAE: what changed in 2026

Before taking any step, keep these dated facts in mind, each with its source:

| Date | Event (source) | Practical consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 7 February 2026 | The APS agency announces that Algeria is starting the denunciation of the air services agreement signed with the UAE in 2013. The agreement provides for 12 months' notice before taking effect (Algerian press). | On 13 February, Emirates announces that its last scheduled flight from Algiers will leave on 3 February 2027 (The National, Khaleej Times) |
| 28 February 2026 | Air Algérie suspends its flights to Dubai, Doha and Amman because of tensions in the Middle East, then extends the suspension "until further notice" (Radio Algérie) | No more direct Air Algérie flight to Dubai |
| March 2026 | According to the aviation press (September 2026), the last Emirates flight between Dubai and Algiers | No Emirati airline has served Algiers since, according to the same sources |
| 10 September 2026 | Algeria's Ministry of Foreign Affairs announces the break of diplomatic relations with the UAE; the Emirati ambassador is summoned and has 48 hours to comply (Al Jazeera, Euronews) | Consular services affected on both sides |
| 11 September 2026 | Algeria's Ministry of National Defence closes the airspace to civil and military aircraft registered in the UAE (APS). Commercial flights to and from Algiers are exempted until the end of 2026 (Al Jazeera, Euronews). | Flights between the two countries go through a third country |

The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs said it hoped the decision would be temporary and expressed its esteem for the Algerian community living in the UAE (Al Jazeera, Khaleej Times, 10 September 2026). As of 27 November 2026, no date for normalisation has been announced: build your plan on the assumption that the situation lasts, then adapt it if it changes.

## Dubai visa for Algerians: mandatory before departure

The Algerian passport is on neither the list of visa-exempt nationalities nor the list entitled to a visa on arrival (lists published by the Emirati press in June 2026, based on official announcements; the list may change). You therefore need a visa issued **before** boarding, with a passport valid for at least six months. As of 27 November 2026, we found no official Emirati announcement suspending visas for Algerians.

The official channels given by u.ae:

- an Emirati airline (Emirates, Etihad, flydubai) if you fly with it;
- a licensed hotel or travel agency, if you book through them;
- the ICP portal or Dubai's GDRFA portal.

UAE embassies do not issue tourist visas. With the diplomatic break, be even more wary of "agencies" that promise a visa through contacts: these are often scams.

### The entry visas that exist

- **Short tourist visa**: requested through an airline, a hotel, an agency or online.
- **5-year multiple-entry tourist visa**: open to all nationalities, no sponsor, with a bank balance of USD 4,000 or equivalent over the last six months, health insurance valid in the UAE and a return ticket. Stays of 90 days, extendable once, within a limit of 180 days a year.
- **Job-seeker visa**: 60, 90 or 120 days, single entry, no sponsor, for bachelor's degree holders in MOHRE skill levels 1 to 3 or recent graduates of a top 500 university (conditions detailed on u.ae).
- **Family visit visa**: a relative resident in the UAE can invite you, under the GDRFA's conditions.

### French-Algerians and residence permit holders

With a French, Belgian or Canadian passport, you enter the UAE without a prior visa. It is advisable to use the same passport on entry, exit and for your residence file. However, a residence card in France does not give an Algerian passport the right to a visa on arrival: that scheme is reserved for a list of nationalities (India, then since June 2026 Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines, Kenya and South Africa, according to the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs) which does not include Algeria.

## Staying in Dubai: residence visas

To work and live in Dubai, you need a residence visa and the Emirates ID. You get it as an employee (the employer applies), as a partner in a company, as a freelancer, through a resident family member, as a student, or through the Golden Visa for certain profiles. ## Legalising your Algerian documents for the UAE

### Apostille or legalisation?

Since **9 July 2026**, Algeria applies the Hague Apostille Convention (apostille.gov.dz platform). But an apostille is only valid between member countries, and **the UAE is not one of them**. For a diploma or civil-status document intended for the UAE, classic legalisation remains the rule:

1. **Legalisation by Algeria's Ministry of Foreign Affairs**, in Algiers or, since February 2026, in its regional annexes in Constantine, Oran and Ouargla.
2. **UAE attestation**, in principle by the UAE embassy in the country of issue, then recognition by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

### The 2026 problem

Step 2 used to go through the UAE Embassy in Algiers, whose ambassador was asked to leave the country in September 2026. As of 27 November 2026, the embassy's page on mofa.gov.ae still mentions an attestation service and shows no suspension notice, but we found no confirmation that this service still works. Before you leave:

- have your documents legalised at Algeria's Ministry of Foreign Affairs in every case;
- log in to the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs attestation service (mofa.gov.ae, with UAE Pass), choose Algeria as the country of issue and see which option is offered;
- if no option appears, call the ministry's call centre (80044444 from within the UAE) and ask what to do;
- keep your originals, unlaminated: a laminated document cannot be attested.

**Algerians in France or Canada**: a diploma earned in France follows the French chain, then the UAE Embassy in Paris. These documents are not affected by the situation in Algiers.

## Embassy, consulate and consular registration

- **UAE Embassy in Algeria**: rue de Carthage, Hydra, Algiers. Its actual operation since the break of 10 September 2026 is not specified by any official source: find out before taking any step.
- **Algerian missions in the UAE**: Algeria had an embassy in Abu Dhabi and a consulate general in Dubai. According to the Algerian press (10 September 2026), the break entails the closure of the diplomatic and consular missions of both countries; we found no official communiqué on the fate of the Consulate General in Dubai. Check the website of Algeria's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (mfa.gov.dz) to learn which post now handles procedures for Algerians in the UAE, notably passport renewal.

Practical advice: renew your passport in Algeria before leaving if it expires in less than two or three years. Renewing it from the UAE could become complicated.

## Transferring your money: the Bank of Algeria rules

The Algerian dinar is not freely convertible, and transfers abroad are strictly controlled by the Bank of Algeria. The **tourist allowance** set by the Bank of Algeria is EUR 750 once per calendar year for residents aged 19 and over, and EUR 300 for a minor aged 12 to 19 (two children at most per family), according to APS. Since instruction no. 07-2026 of July 2026, it is paid onto a named international payment card linked to a foreign-currency account, and the beneficiary must also hold a dinar account. The other conditions (supporting documents required, the case of dual nationals) should be confirmed with your bank.

What this means for your move:

- the tourist allowance is for travel, not for financing a relocation;
- for larger amounts, ask your bank what is authorised before you leave;
- avoid informal exchange networks: they are illegal and you would have no proof of the origin of funds, which UAE banks require;
- in the UAE, any cash amount above AED 60,000 (or the equivalent) must be declared on entry through the ICP's Afseh system.

Once you have your residence, open a local account to receive your salary: see our guide to [opening a bank account in Dubai](/en/open-bank-account-dubai/).

## Tax: the Algeria–UAE treaty

A tax treaty between Algeria and the UAE was signed on 24 April 2001. The table filed by Algeria with the OECD and the UAE Ministry of Finance's list also give the date of 25 June 2004, probably that of entry into force (not confirmed from the text of the treaty). The UAE does not tax individuals' salaries. If you keep income in Algeria, or if you remain an Algerian tax resident, you may remain taxable there. The diplomatic break does not in itself amount to denouncing the treaty, but ask for confirmation from Algeria's tax authority (Direction Générale des Impôts) or a tax adviser.

## Flights between Algeria and Dubai

- **Emirates** used to link Algiers and Dubai every day. On 13 February 2026, the airline announced its last scheduled flight from Algiers for 3 February 2027. According to the aviation press (September 2026), no Emirati airline has served Algiers since March 2026. The exemption granted to commercial flights to and from Algiers runs until the end of 2026.
- **Air Algérie** suspended its flights to Dubai on 28 February 2026, because of tensions in the Middle East, and had announced no resumption date as of September 2026.
- **In practice**: plan a connection through a third country (for example Doha, Istanbul, Cairo or Tunis) and check schedules with the airlines before booking.

## Working in Dubai as an Algerian: contract and scams

Engineers, doctors, teachers, finance and digital profiles: Algerian skills find their place in Dubai. The context nevertheless makes vigilance even more necessary.

What UAE labour law provides:

- an employer may only have you work after obtaining a work permit from MOHRE;
- recruitment, travel, visa and residence costs are borne by the employer, never by the employee;
- contracts are fixed-term, and the probation period does not exceed six months;
- keep a copy of the signed offer and contact MOHRE if the post or the salary is not what was promised.

The warning signs: a "placement office" that asks for money, a promise of an "easy" visa despite the diplomatic break, an offer with no verifiable company name, advice to come on a tourist visa to "sort it out afterwards". For the job market and salaries, read our guide to [working in Dubai](/en/working-in-dubai/).

## Studying in Dubai as an Algerian

The university that admits you sponsors your student visa. The steps:

1. Get an admission (most programmes are in English, a language test is often required).
2. Have the baccalaureate and transcripts legalised, then attested (see above: allow much more time in 2026).
3. Request the equivalence of the secondary-school diploma from the UAE Ministry of Education, online through UAE Pass. Documents in a language other than Arabic or English must be translated by a licensed translator.
4. Let the university file the visa application.

More details in our guide to [studying in Dubai](/en/study-in-dubai/).

## Life on the ground and community

Algerians settled in the UAE mix with the Moroccan, Tunisian and more broadly Arabic- and French-speaking communities: Arabic is the official language, English dominates at work and French opens the door to Dubai's many francophone networks. The Algerian driving licence cannot be directly exchanged in Dubai: according to the RTA (page updated on 24 November 2026), 60 countries are on the exchange list and South Africa is the only African country. Plan lessons at a driving school and check the up-to-date list on rta.ae.

## Checklist before leaving Algeria

1. Passport valid for a long time (renew it before leaving if necessary).
2. Entry visa obtained through an official channel (ICP, GDRFA, Emirati airline, licensed hotel or agency).
3. Documents legalised at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and UAE attestation option checked on mofa.gov.ae.
4. Translations into Arabic or English.
5. Itinerary with a connection, changeable ticket.
6. Review with your bank of the permitted transfers.
7. Verified job offer, with no fees to pay.
8. Registration with the Algerian mission designated for the UAE.
