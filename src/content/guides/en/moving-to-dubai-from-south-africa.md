## Moving to Dubai from South Africa: what is specific to South Africans

The UAE steps (residence visa, medical test, Emirates ID, health insurance, bank account) are shared by all nationalities and covered in our guide to [moving to Dubai](/en/moving-to-dubai/). What is specific to South Africa is how you leave the South African tax and exchange-control system and how your documents are recognised.

| Step | Body | When |
|---|---|---|
| Choose your residence route | Employer, company, investment or family | Months before |
| Decide your tax residency position | SARS | Before departure |
| Plan transfers and allowances | Authorised dealer bank, SARB rules | Before departure |
| Legalise documents | South African authorities, UAE mission | Before departure |
| Entry, residence visa, Emirates ID, insurance | UAE authorities | First weeks |

## Dubai visa for South Africans

The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs lists South Africa as **visa required**. There is one exception. On 25 June 2026, the Ministry extended its visa-on-arrival scheme to South Africa, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines and Kenya, for citizens with an ordinary passport who hold a valid residence permit issued by the United States, an EU member state, the United Kingdom, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand or Canada, and their accompanying family members. Two options are offered: **14 days for 100 AED**, extendable once, or **60 days for 250 AED**, not extendable. An overstay is fined 50 AED a day.

If you live in South Africa with no such permit, apply for the visa before you travel through an airline, a hotel, a licensed agency or the ICP and GDRFA platforms. A visit does not allow you to work: for a job, the employer sponsors the entry permit and residence.

## From visit to residence

The routes are the same as for other nationalities: employer sponsorship, a company you own, a Golden Visa category or a relative who is resident. ## Ceasing South African tax residency

Many South Africans still say "financial emigration". That process is gone, and the question that matters is your **tax residency**.

### How SARS looks at it

According to SARS, you can cease to be a South African tax resident in three ways:

1. **You cease to be ordinarily resident.** This is a factual question: whether you intend to stop making South Africa your real home. SARS looks at objective factors such as the type of visa you used abroad, permanent residence abroad, property, family, business interests, return visits and whether you hold a foreign tax residence certificate.
2. **Physical presence abroad.** You cease to be resident once you have been outside the Republic for a continuous period of at least **330 full days**, and you are treated as having ceased from the day you left.
3. **Residence under a double tax agreement.** If a treaty makes you a resident of another country, you also cease to be a South African resident, and SARS will usually ask for a certificate of tax residence from the foreign authority.

### What you must do

Update your status with the **RAV01** form on eFiling, entering the cessation date, and submit the declaration with supporting documents through eFiling or SOQS: a signed declaration, a detailed motivation letter, passport entry and exit records, and, depending on your basis, visas and proof of permanent residence abroad or a foreign tax residence certificate. SARS can decline a declaration if you do not meet the criteria or lack documents. If you come back and become resident again, you record the reinstatement date.

### Exit tax: the deemed disposal

On ceasing residency, SARS states that a **deemed disposal for capital gains tax** takes place on your worldwide assets, except South African immovable property. After that, you are taxed in South Africa only on South African-source income. Have the gain calculated before you leave if you hold shares, a business or a portfolio. SARS also publishes guides on retirement funds and tax directives: ask a South African tax practitioner.

### The UAE side

The UAE does not tax the personal income of individuals; see [tax in Dubai](/en/tax-in-dubai/). If you rely on UAE residence as part of your SARS declaration, you will need a certificate: our page on the [UAE tax residency certificate](/en/uae-tax-residency-certificate/) explains how the Federal Tax Authority issues it, and what it does not prove.

## Exchange control: what the Reserve Bank allows

The South African Reserve Bank explains the rules for individuals on its FAQ page:

| Allowance | Amount | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Single discretionary allowance | R1 million per adult per calendar year | Any legal purpose abroad; valid ID; ticket for travel outside the Common Monetary Area |
| Foreign investment allowance | Up to R10 million per calendar year | Taxpayer in good standing; SARS tax compliance verification above the R1 million portion |
| After ceasing tax residency | Up to R10 million per calendar year, plus a once-off R1 million travel allowance in the year of cessation | SARS tax compliance status; the travel allowance cannot be combined with unused SDA |

Transfers must go to your own bank account abroad, not to a third party, through an authorised dealer. Above R10 million, FinSurv approval is required. Lump-sum retirement withdrawals are allowed only after at least three consecutive years of non-tax residence, with the right compliance status and fund documents. The SARB notes that the concept of emigration was phased out from 1 March 2021. These rules are updated: check them with your authorised dealer before you transfer.

## Getting South African documents recognised

South Africa has been a party to the Apostille Convention since 1995, but the UAE does not appear in the Hague Conference status table (update of 30 June 2026). An apostille alone is therefore not enough: confirm the full chain with the UAE mission that covers South Africa (the Ministry list shows a South African embassy in Abu Dhabi and a consulate in Dubai, and does not give us the UAE mission in South Africa) before you send documents. You will typically need degrees, marriage and birth certificates and, for certain jobs, a police clearance. Where the receiving body asks for it, the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs attests the document once you are in the UAE.

## Common mistakes when moving from South Africa

- **Treating "financial emigration" as the goal.** It no longer exists. The step that counts is your SARS declaration, and your transfers depend on your tax compliance status.
- **Counting 330 days loosely.** The physical presence basis requires a continuous period of at least 330 full days outside the Republic. Short returns can change the count.
- **Forgetting the deemed disposal.** The capital gains tax on your worldwide assets (except South African immovable property) can be significant. Get a calculation before you leave.
- **Moving money through a third party.** The SARB requires funds to go to your own account abroad through an authorised dealer.
- **Expecting a retirement fund payout on day one.** Lump-sum withdrawals are allowed only after three consecutive years of non-tax residence and with the right documents.
- **Assuming the visa on arrival is for everyone.** It applies only to citizens holding a residence permit from the approved countries.

2. Residence route chosen (job, company, family or investment).
