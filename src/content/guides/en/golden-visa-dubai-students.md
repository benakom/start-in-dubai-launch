A long-term residence with no sponsor is a strong prize for a young graduate, and the UAE does offer one to its best students. But the criteria are narrow, they come with a **two-year window after graduation**, and the official pages disagree on small points such as the duration. This guide sets out what the ICP, the GDRFA and u.ae say, so you can tell quickly whether you qualify.

Facts were consulted on 28 November 2026. Conditions can change: confirm on the official page before you plan around them. For the ordinary student residence visa, see our guide to the [student visa in Dubai](/en/student-visa-dubai/).

## What is the Golden Visa for students?

It is one category of the federal Golden Residency. The ICP calls it the Golden Residency for **outstanding students and graduates**, and u.ae adds that the file rests on certificates of excellence and recommendation letters from the school or university. The visa is a residence permit: it does not give citizenship and does not replace your studies.

The Golden Visa has other categories (investors, entrepreneurs, specialised talents, employees). For those, read our main [Golden Visa guide](/en/golden-visa-dubai/) and our guide to the [Golden Visa without property](/en/golden-visa-dubai-without-property/).

## Who qualifies: three profiles

### 1. Top secondary school pupils

According to the ICP and the GDRFA:

- a Grade 12 certificate from a **government school** or a **private school licensed** by the Ministry of Education or the local education authority;
- a minimum grade of **95%** or its equivalent;
- the certificate **attested** by the Ministry of Education.

The ICP specifies "graduating from schools in the UAE". If you studied abroad and are leaving school there, this profile is not the one for you.

### 2. Top graduates of UAE universities

- a **bachelor's, master's or doctoral** degree from a university licensed by the Ministry of Education;
- the university must be in **Category A or Category B** of the Ministry of Education's classification;
- a cumulative GPA of at least **3.5 out of 4.0** (Category A) or **3.8 out of 4.0** (Category B);
- graduated **no more than two years ago**;
- certificate attested by the Ministry of Education (ICP), which the GDRFA words as the Ministry of Higher Education.

### 3. Top graduates of foreign universities

- the university is among the **top 100 in the world**, according to an international ranking recognised by the Ministry of Education;
- a cumulative GPA of at least **3.5** (the ICP page states it for bachelor's degrees);
- graduated **no more than two years ago**;
- certificate attested by the ministry.

The ICP describes the criteria for bachelor's graduates abroad. If you hold a master's degree or a doctorate from a foreign university, ask the ICP or the GDRFA which criteria apply to you before you spend money on attestations.

### Quick eligibility table

| Your profile | Threshold | Time limit | Where the file starts |
|---|---|---|---|
| UAE school leaver | 95% or equivalent | Not stated on the pages | Ministry approval via the ICP system |
| UAE university graduate (Category A) | GPA 3.5 / 4.0 | 2 years after graduation | Ministry approval via the ICP system |
| UAE university graduate (Category B) | GPA 3.8 / 4.0 | 2 years after graduation | Ministry approval via the ICP system |
| Foreign university graduate (top 100) | GPA 3.5 (bachelor's) | 2 years after graduation | Ministry approval via the ICP system |

### What does not qualify

- a good result below the threshold, or a degree from a university outside the top 100 (foreign) or outside Category A and B (UAE);
- a degree obtained more than two years before the application;
- an unattested certificate, or one that the Ministry of Education does not recognise;
- an assumption that your university "is well known": the ministry's list and classification decide, not a newspaper ranking.

## Duration: 5 or 10 years?

The official pages do not agree:

| Source | Duration stated |
|---|---|
| ICP | 10 years for top secondary school pupils and for top graduates of accredited UAE universities |
| GDRFA Dubai | 10 years, not renewed |
| u.ae (updated 28 July 2026) | 5 years for high school achievers and 10 years for top university students |

The authority that issues your permit sets its duration. If you live in Dubai, that is the GDRFA. Ask for the confirmed duration at the approval stage and note it in your plan. The GDRFA states that the student Golden Visa **is not renewed** when it expires.

## How the application works

The GDRFA states a key point: applications **cannot be submitted** without approval from the relevant ministry via the ICP system, or nomination by the competent local authorities. For Dubai, student approval requires a residence file issued solely by the Emirate of Dubai. The pages do not describe the nomination process itself, so you will need to ask your school, your university or the GDRFA how to be put forward.

1. **Check the threshold** for your profile (grade, GPA, university category or ranking, graduation date).
2. **Get your certificate attested** by the Ministry of Education (and by the Ministry of Higher Education if the GDRFA asks). For documents from abroad, use the official chain: authentication in the issuing country, then attestation by a UAE embassy or consulate.
3. **Ask for ministry approval** through the ICP system, or the nomination, via your institution or the GDRFA.
4. **Prepare the documents** the GDRFA lists: passport copy, the certificate, a copy of your Dubai-issued residence permit where required, and the ICP approval.
5. **Apply online** (UAE Pass or username, available 24/7) or through an Amer service centre, and pay the fees.
6. **Complete the residence formalities** (medical test, Emirates ID, insurance) and keep the expiry date.

## Official fees

The GDRFA service sheet lists these amounts, in AED:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Residence permit | 1,100 AED |
| Knowledge Dirham | 10 AED |
| Innovation Dirham | 10 AED |
| Delivery | 20 AED |
| Fee for applying inside the country | 500 AED |
| Residence longer than two years | The issuance fee increases by 100 AED per year |

Expected completion time: **5 days**. The GDRFA says the total may vary with your circumstances. Insurance and the Emirates ID cost extra: our guide to the [Emirates ID](/en/blog/emirates-id/) lists them.

## Family: who can you sponsor?

The GDRFA draws a clear line:

- a **top school leaver** may sponsor **parents only**, not brothers or sisters;
- a **university student or graduate** may sponsor a **spouse and children**, provided they show an employment contract, a steady monthly income and housing, under the applicable family sponsorship rules.

For parents in particular, see our guide to [sponsoring parents in Dubai](/en/sponsor-parents-dubai-visa/).

## What happens after the Golden Visa?

Because the student permit is not renewed, think of it as a long runway, not a permanent status. Near the end, you will need another basis for residence. The usual options are:

- a job with an employer that sponsors you, or the Golden Visa for skilled employees (see [Golden Visa for professionals and employees](/en/golden-visa-dubai-employees/));
- a company, once you are ready to trade: see [company formation in Dubai](/en/company-formation-dubai/);
- a freelance route: [freelance visa in Dubai](/en/freelance-visa-dubai/) and the [UAE Green Visa for freelancers](/en/uae-green-visa-freelancers/).

If you are still choosing where to study, our guides to [studying in Dubai](/en/study-in-dubai/) and [cheapest universities in Dubai](/en/cheapest-universities-dubai/) cover the admission side.

## How to read the criteria before you spend money

Three questions settle most cases:

1. **Which profile are you?** A pupil leaving a UAE school, a graduate of a UAE university, or a graduate of a foreign university. The thresholds are different for each.
2. **Is the number met in black and white?** 95%, 3.5 or 3.8 are minimums that the ministry checks against your attested certificate. Do not round up.
3. **Is the clock still running?** For graduates, the ICP counts two years from graduation to the application date. If you are close to the limit, ask the GDRFA how the date is counted before you begin the attestation chain.

If your university is outside the UAE, also find out whether it appears in the ranking recognised by the Ministry of Education. The pages we used do not publish the list, so this is a question for the ministry, the ICP or the GDRFA, not for an agent.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Waiting more than two years after graduation.** The ICP sets a two-year limit for graduates.
- **Assuming 94% is close enough.** The threshold is a minimum.
- **Skipping the attestation.** An unattested certificate blocks the file.
- **Paying someone who "guarantees" the visa.** Ministry approval decides. No agent controls it.
- **Mixing up the student visa and the Golden Visa.** The student residence visa is sponsored by your university and ends with your studies.

## Checklist

- [ ] My grade or GPA meets the threshold in black and white
- [ ] My graduation date is within two years
