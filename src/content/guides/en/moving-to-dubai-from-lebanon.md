This guide is for Lebanese citizens in Lebanon and in the diaspora (France, Canada, the United States, the Gulf, Africa) who are preparing a move to Dubai. It covers visa, papers, money, work and study, in the order the questions come up. **Checked on 28 November 2026.** Visa rules and Lebanon's banking situation change: always confirm on the official sources listed at the bottom, and be wary of any figure that is not there.

## Moving to Dubai from Lebanon: the steps

| Step | Where | When |
|---|---|---|
| Choose the residence route: job, company, study, family, Golden Visa | UAE | 3 to 6 months before |
| Confirm the entry visa for your passport | UAE official tool or embassy | Before buying a ticket |
| Authenticate and attest documents | Lebanon, then UAE embassy in Beirut | Before departure |
| Ask your bank for a written answer on transfers | Lebanon | Before departure |
| Have your tax residence validated | Lebanon | Before departure |
| Residence visa, medical test, Emirates ID, health insurance | UAE | First weeks |

For the overview that applies to every nationality, see [moving to Dubai](/en/moving-to-dubai/).

## Dubai visa for Lebanese citizens: the entry rule

We did not find, on an official Emirati page, the rule that applies to a Lebanese passport. From the information available, a visa obtained before departure is generally required, with no visa on arrival. **Do not rely on third-party sites**: use the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs entry-conditions tool or contact the UAE embassy in Beirut before you buy a ticket.

For all visitors, the Ministry states a stay of up to 90 days within a 180-day period and a passport valid for at least six months on entry (three months if you hold a residence permit). According to u.ae, a tourist visa can be requested through an Emirati airline, a hotel or a licensed agency, or through the official portals (ICP, GDRFA).

### Which entry visa should you pick?

| Your plan | Suitable visa | Key points |
|---|---|---|
| Discover Dubai, scout the city | Tourist visa | Requested through the airline, hotel, agency or online |
| Come often while you prepare | 5-year multiple-entry tourist visa | Financial and insurance conditions set by u.ae: re-read the page before applying |
| Look for work on the spot | [Job seeker visa](/en/job-seeker-visa-dubai/) (60, 90 or 120 days) | Skill level and financial guarantee required, see u.ae |
| Visit a resident relative | Family visit visa | Requested by the resident |

### Dual nationals

If you hold a second passport (European, Canadian, American or Gulf), the entry rule depends on the passport you travel with. Choose one and keep it for entry, exit and your residence file: mixing two passports complicates checks.

## Residence visas for living in Dubai

A visit visa never allows you to work. To stay, you need a residence visa and the Emirates ID, obtained through one of these routes:

- an **employer**, who requests the work permit then the residence: see [working in Dubai](/en/working-in-dubai/);
- your **own company**: see [company formation in Dubai](/en/company-formation-dubai/);
- a **freelance permit** for consultants, developers, creatives or trainers: see the [freelance visa guide](/en/freelance-visa-dubai/);
- a **resident family member** who sponsors you: see [moving to Dubai with family](/en/moving-to-dubai-with-family/);
- a **university**, for students: [study in Dubai](/en/study-in-dubai/);
- the **Golden Visa**, for certain investors and talents: [Golden Visa guide](/en/golden-visa-dubai/).

The steps from entry permit to Emirates ID are in our [residence visa guide](/en/residence-visa-dubai/). The [Emirates ID](/en/blog/emirates-id/) is then used for banking, tenancy and your phone line.

## Authenticating your documents in Lebanon

The UAE requires an authentication chain for documents issued abroad. At a minimum, prepare your degrees and transcripts, your marriage certificate and your children's civil-status extracts if they will join you.

### Step 1: Lebanon's Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Emigrants

According to the UAE embassy in Beirut, documents issued in Lebanon must first be attested by the Lebanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Emigrants. Legal acts, such as a power of attorney, must be signed before a Lebanese notary or competent authority, then attested by the same ministry. Check the opening hours and required papers with the ministry before you travel.

### Step 2: attestation by the UAE embassy in Beirut

The embassy uses the digital service of the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs:

1. Log in to the ministry's site with UAE Pass (non-residents can register as visitors with a few details).
2. Choose the Lebanese Republic as the country of issue.
3. Pay the fees online, then a courier collects the original.
4. The attested document is delivered back to you.

One file covers the embassy and the UAE ministry. The fees are not shown on the embassy page we consulted: they are displayed when you apply.

### Rules that make files fail

- **Original required**: the embassy wants the physical original.
- **No laminated documents.**
- **Stamp dates**: in general less than a year old; less than six months for certified copies of study documents; less than three months for prescriptions.
- **Company documents**: articles and memoranda are attested separately, and a power of attorney with commercial content is treated as a commercial document.
- **No bulk apostille**: the embassy states that documents apostilled in bulk are not accepted, and each document must be done individually.
- **Announced timing**: courier collection within 24 hours of the request, delivery in 24 to 48 hours (48 to 72 hours in remote areas), on top of the Lebanese ministry's time.

Embassy contact: BeirutEMB@mofa.gov.ae. For delivery problems: attestationuaeleb@vfsglobal.com.

**Lebanese abroad**: a document issued in France or Canada follows the chain of the issuing country, then the UAE embassy in that country. It does not go through Beirut.

## Money: transferring from Lebanon

Lebanon's banking situation has changed since the 2019 crisis, and withdrawal and transfer rules depend on the type of account and on the circulars of the Banque du Liban. We deliberately publish no limit: it varies and must be confirmed in writing by your bank. In practice:

- ask your bank what is allowed for your account (foreign currency or Lebanese pounds) and for which type of transfer;
- if you hold funds outside Lebanon, build the origin-of-funds file before you open a UAE account;
- keep supporting documents (statements, tax notices, sale deeds): the UAE bank will ask for them;
- cash above the threshold set by the authorities must be declared on entry to the UAE: check the threshold with the ICP or customs.

For the next step, see [how to open a bank account in Dubai](/en/open-bank-account-dubai/) and [why UAE banks reject accounts](/en/blog/uae-bank-account-rejected-reasons/).

## Tax: Lebanon and the UAE

The UAE does not tax personal salaries: see [tax in Dubai](/en/tax-in-dubai/). You may remain taxable in Lebanon if you keep income or assets there. On the UAE Ministry of Finance page we consulted, we found no confirmation of a double taxation treaty with Lebanon, and a commercial tax summary of Lebanon lists the UAE among its treaty partners. These two sources do not agree, so do not count on a treaty before checking the UAE Ministry of Finance treaties dashboard. Have your tax residence validated by a tax adviser, and on the Emirati side request the tax residency certificate from the Federal Tax Authority: see our [UAE tax residency certificate](/en/uae-tax-residency-certificate/) guide.

## Working in Dubai as a Lebanese citizen

Dubai employers recruit in many sectors, and Lebanese professionals apply like everyone else. UAE labour law applies to everyone in the same way:

- the employer must obtain a MOHRE work permit before you start;
- the contract is fixed-term and renewable;
- **recruitment, visa and residence costs are paid by the employer**;
- keep a copy of the signed offer: it is your reference in case of dispute.

### Scams to spot

- fees requested for a visa, a "file" or a reserved position;
- an offer received only on social networks, with no identifiable company;
- advice to come on a tourist visa and "regularise" later;
- a contract that differs from the signed offer.

See our guide to [Dubai job scams](/en/blog/dubai-job-scams/) and the [cost of living in Dubai](/en/cost-of-living-dubai/) to compare any offer with real costs.

## Studying in Dubai from Lebanon

The university that admits you sponsors your student visa. Prepare the baccalaureate or secondary-school leaving certificate, transcripts, an identity document and the admission letter. Study documents follow the chain above, with stamps less than six months old for certified copies. See [study in Dubai](/en/study-in-dubai/).

## Lebanese representation in the UAE

Ask Lebanon's Ministry of Foreign Affairs which Lebanese embassy covers the UAE and whether there is a presence in Dubai, and register on arrival for consular services. Renew your passport before leaving if it expires in less than two years.

## Checklist before leaving Lebanon

1. Passport valid for several years.
2. Entry visa confirmed on the official tool or with the embassy.
3. Documents authenticated at the Lebanese ministry, then attested by the UAE embassy (original, not laminated).
4. Written answer from your bank on transfers.
5. Opinion of a tax adviser on your tax residence.
6. Verified job offer with no fees to pay, or a university admission.
7. Health insurance valid in the UAE from arrival ([health insurance guide](/en/blog/health-insurance-dubai/)).
