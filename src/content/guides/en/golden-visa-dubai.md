## What is the Dubai Golden Visa?

The Golden Visa (*Golden Residency*) is a long-term residence permit issued by the United Arab Emirates. People often talk about the "Dubai golden visa", but it is a federal scheme: it is valid across the whole country, whether you live in Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Sharjah.

What sets it apart from a standard residence visa:

- **A long duration:** 5 or 10 years depending on the category, renewable as long as you meet the conditions. Note that official sources do not always agree: the federal portal u.ae (page updated on 28 July 2026) states 5 years for property and for entrepreneurs, while the GDRFA in Dubai and the Dubai Land Department announce 10 years for the same routes. The duration that prevails is the one stated by the authority that issues your visa (GDRFA in Dubai, ICP in the other emirates).
- **No sponsor:** you need neither an employer nor a company to stay in the country. You are your own "sponsor".
- **Freedom to work, study and invest** in the UAE.

If you are new to how visas work in the UAE, start with our guide to the [residence visa in Dubai](/en/residence-visa-dubai/): it explains the standard visas (employment, company, family), which are enough for many relocation projects.

## Golden Visa Dubai: requirements by category

The conditions below come from the ICP (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security) and the GDRFA in Dubai. They change regularly: always check the official page for your category before submitting an application.

### Property investors: from 2 million dirhams

This is the best-known route. You must hold in your own name one or more properties with a total value of at least **2 million dirhams (AED)**, purchase value according to the Dubai Land Department. Off-plan properties bought from an approved developer can be taken into account under conditions. For a property financed by a mortgage, the DLD requires a letter from the bank stating the amount already paid and the remaining balance. For a jointly owned property, the GDRFA specifies that the applicant's share must itself reach 2 million dirhams. The property must not be resold during the validity of the visa.

On duration, the GDRFA and the DLD speak of a 10-year residence, while u.ae states 5 years for property investments. Have the duration confirmed by the DLD or the GDRFA when you apply.

### Financial investors and company partners

The ICP accepts one of these situations (with full health insurance):

- a deposit of at least 2 million dirhams in an investment fund or a national bank;
- a company with capital of at least 2 million dirhams, or a partner's share of at least that amount;
- a company that pays at least 250,000 AED in federal taxes a year, or a stake corresponding to that amount.

The funds must belong to you personally: borrowed capital is not accepted and the origin of the funds must be justified.

### Entrepreneurs

The ICP provides three routes, to choose from:

- an SME registered in the UAE, in an approved sector, with annual revenue of at least **1 million dirhams**;
- a project approved by an accredited incubator, the Ministry of Economy or a competent local authority;
- having founded one or more companies that were sold for at least **7 million dirhams** in total.

In Dubai, the GDRFA also requires a nomination by the Dubai Future Foundation. If your project involves setting up a company, read our guide to [company formation in Dubai](/en/company-formation-dubai/) first. The three routes, the nomination and the fees are detailed in our page on the [Golden Visa for entrepreneurs](/en/golden-visa-dubai-entrepreneurs/).

### Skilled employees: the salary condition

An employee can obtain the Golden Visa without investing, if they meet all of these conditions:

- a position classified at **level 1 or 2** by the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE), for example executive or specialist;
- a valid work permit and employment contract in the UAE;
- at least a **bachelor's degree** or equivalent;
- a monthly salary of at least **30,000 AED** (a condition also stated on the official Abu Dhabi portal);
- full health insurance.

The point that causes the most refusals is the nature of the salary counted. The official pages speak of a monthly salary of at least 30,000 AED without specifying whether it means the basic salary; since 2024, the Emirati press has reported that only the basic salary, excluding allowances, is taken into account. Have it confirmed by the ICP or the GDRFA before applying. Documents, MOHRE levels and the Green Visa alternative are covered in our page on the [Golden Visa for professionals and employees](/en/golden-visa-dubai-employees/).

### Talents, scientists, creatives and athletes

This category covers doctors, scientists, inventors, PhD holders, specialists in priority sectors, artists and creatives, digital talents and top athletes. There is no financial threshold: you need a **recommendation** from the competent body (Emirates Scientists Council, Ministry of Culture, Ministry of Health, Ministry of Education, local authorities…) and evidence of your track record (awards, publications, experience, recognition).

### Outstanding students and graduates

According to the ICP:

- **High school students:** a secondary school leaving certificate (Grade 12) from an approved school with an average of at least 95%, attested by the Ministry of Education.
- **Graduates of a university ranked in the world top 100:** a GPA of at least 3.5 at bachelor's level, degree obtained less than two years ago.
- **Graduates of a UAE university** (category A or B): an average of at least 3.5 out of 4 (category A) or 3.8 out of 4 (category B), degree obtained less than two years ago.

The duration is 5 or 10 years depending on the profile: u.ae states 5 years for high school students and 10 years for top university graduates. If you are simply preparing to study in the UAE, the [student visa in Dubai](/en/student-visa-dubai/) is the normal route.

### Humanitarian and frontline profiles

Humanitarian pioneers (at least five years in an international, regional or non-profit organisation, or financial support of at least 2 million dirhams to humanitarian causes, with a university degree) and frontline professionals may also be eligible.

## Golden Visa benefits

| Benefit | What the ICP provides |
|---|---|
| Duration | 5 or 10 years depending on the category (to be confirmed with the issuing authority), renewable |
| Sponsor | None: you are autonomous |
| Family | Spouse, children of any age, and parents |
| Domestic staff | Possibility to sponsor domestic workers |
| Stays abroad | The visa remains valid beyond six months outside the country |
| Death of the holder | The family can stay until their permits expire |
| Application from abroad | 6-month multiple-entry visa, renewable once |

The family benefit is often decisive. With a standard visa, sons can in principle only be sponsored up to age 25; with the Golden Visa, the ICP states that children can be sponsored regardless of age. To organise life on the ground (schools, housing, insurance), see our guide to [moving to Dubai with your family](/en/moving-to-dubai-with-family/), and [sponsoring parents in Dubai](/en/sponsor-parents-dubai-visa/) for what is confirmed about parents.

## Golden Visa Dubai: official prices and fees

There is no "single price". Fees depend on the category, the emirate and the number of people. Here are the orders of magnitude published by the Dubai authorities:

- **Property route (Dubai Land Department):** about **9,885 AED** in total for the applicant (9,884.75 AED in the DLD schedule), which includes the medical test (700 AED), the 10-year Emirates ID (1,153 AED), confirmation of the residence permit, DLD fees and administrative fees.
- **Family:** the DLD schedule shows about 5,775 AED per 10-year residence permit for a spouse, child or parent, plus about 319 AED to open the sponsorship file and 100 AED per person sponsored.
- **Other categories (GDRFA):** the GDRFA publishes base fees (1,100 AED for the permit, plus ancillary fees and 500 AED if the application is made from within the UAE), to which the medical test and the Emirates ID are added.

Also plan for **health insurance**, mandatory for the whole duration of the visa, and possible translation or legalisation costs for documents. Be wary of offers advertising a "guaranteed Golden Visa" for a fixed fee: no agency can guarantee a decision that belongs to the authorities.

## The procedure: ICP, GDRFA and Dubai Land Department

### 1. Check your eligibility

The ICP offers an online eligibility check tool. Identify your category and gather the evidence it requires. For creatives, the Ministry of Culture's recommendation is a separate step; for entrepreneurs in Dubai, the nomination goes through the Dubai Future Foundation.

### 2. Submit the application

- **Dubai residents:** through the GDRFA online services or approved centres (Amer centres).
- **Property route in Dubai:** through the Dubai Land Department and its approved centres.
- **Other emirates:** through the ICP platform.

Common documents: passport, compliant photo, proof of the category (title deed, bank statement, employment contract and salary certificate, attested degrees, recommendation letter…), health insurance, and for family, certified marriage certificate and birth certificates.

### 3. Medical test and Emirates ID

As for any residence visa, adult applicants take a medical test, then give fingerprints for the **Emirates ID**, which serves as proof of residence (the visa has no longer been stamped in the passport since 2022).

### 4. Bringing your family

Once your Golden Visa is issued, you can sponsor your spouse, children and parents. Foreign documents generally have to be legalised in your country and then by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

## Common misconceptions about the Golden Visa

- **"The Golden Visa is citizenship."** No, it is a residence permit. It does not give an Emirati passport.
- **"You have to buy a property."** No. Highly skilled employees, entrepreneurs, talents and outstanding students have access to it without property: see [Golden Visa Dubai without property](/en/golden-visa-dubai-without-property/) for every route.
- **"It is for life."** No. It lasts 5 or 10 years depending on the category and is renewed if the conditions are still met. If you resell the property that justified it, you lose the basis of your visa.
- **"With the Golden Visa, I no longer pay tax in my home country."** No. The visa settles the right to reside, not tax residence. Your home country may continue to treat you as a tax resident. Read our guide to [taxes in Dubai](/en/tax-in-dubai/).
- **"A 2-year property owner visa is the same thing."** No. The property owner residence visa (2 years) has different conditions and benefits, without the family advantages and the flexibility of the Golden Visa.
- **"An intermediary can guarantee approval."** No. The final decision belongs to the ICP or the GDRFA, even after a nomination.

## Checklist before submitting your application

1. Identify your category and read its official page (ICP, GDRFA or DLD).
2. Check each numerical threshold (2 million, 30,000 AED salary, average, revenue) with up-to-date supporting documents.
3. Have your degrees attested and your civil status documents legalised in advance.
4. Take out compliant health insurance.
5. Plan the budget for official fees for yourself and each family member.
6. Keep the tax question in your home country in mind before you move.
