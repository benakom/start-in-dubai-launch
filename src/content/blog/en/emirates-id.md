## What exactly is the Emirates ID?

The **Emirates ID** is the official identity card of the United Arab Emirates. It is issued by the **ICP** (Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security). Every citizen and every foreign resident must have one.

According to the official u.ae portal:

- applying is **mandatory** for citizens and residents, who must **carry the card with them** at all times;
- it carries a **15-digit identity number** that stays with you, even if you change visa;
- a company may register your card data but **is not allowed to keep the card**. An employer or landlord who holds on to your Emirates ID is breaking the rules.

Since 2022 the visa sticker has disappeared from passports: the Emirates ID is what proves you are a resident. To understand the different visas that entitle you to one, read our guide to the [residence visa in Dubai](/en/residence-visa-dubai/).

## What is the Emirates ID used for day to day?

You will be asked for it almost everywhere. Without it, settling in stalls.

| Procedure | Why the Emirates ID is required |
|---|---|
| Bank account | Customer identification; an account can be restricted if the card expires |
| Lease and Ejari | Registration of the tenancy contract in the tenant's name |
| Electricity and water (DEWA) | Opening the account for your home |
| Phone line | Mandatory registration of the SIM card |
| UAE Pass | Creating your digital identity for online services |
| Airports | Use of the automatic gates (eGates, smart gates) at several airports |
| Healthcare, schools, public services | Identification in most procedures |

Opening an account is often the first step that depends on it: our guide to [opening a bank account in Dubai](/en/open-bank-account-dubai/) explains what else banks ask for.

## How to get the Emirates ID

### The principle: the card follows the visa

You do not apply for the Emirates ID on its own: it comes with your **residence visa**. The usual path is:

1. **Entry permit or change of status**: your sponsor (employer, company, relative, or yourself for a visa with no sponsor) starts the residence application.
2. **Medical test**: mandatory to obtain or renew a residence visa. In Dubai it is done at a Dubai Health medical fitness centre (blood test, chest X-ray). Results usually arrive within 24 hours, according to the [Dubai government portal](https://www.dubai.ae/living/healthcare/medical-fitness-for-residence-visa).
3. **Emirates ID application**: online on the ICP website or app, or at an authorised typing centre. The ICP states that the application is built into the unified residence form in every emirate except Dubai, where the visa is handled by the GDRFA: in Dubai, your Amer centre or your sponsor will tell you how the two applications follow each other.
4. **Biometrics**: fingerprints, photo and signature at an ICP centre.
5. **Issuing and delivery**: the card is delivered by an authorised courier. With the urgent service, you collect it at an ICP centre.

### The documents required

According to the ICP service page, for a resident:

- a **passport valid for at least 3 months**;
- a recent **photo** that meets ICAO standards (light background, face uncovered);
- **proof of residence** depending on your category (work visa, investor visa, property owner, family…).

The application can be filed by the person themselves if they are an adult, by the head of the family, a legal representative or a guardian.

### Biometrics: from what age?

The ICP and u.ae state that **fingerprints and a signature are mandatory from age 15**, at an ICP service centre. You must attend within the set period, otherwise the application is cancelled.

### Children and newborns

A resident child also has their own Emirates ID, requested with their visa by the parent who sponsors them. For a child of foreign parents born in the UAE, the ICP states that a fine applies beyond **four months after birth** if the formalities have not been completed. Do not wait: you first need the child's birth certificate and passport, then their residence visa. ## Emirates ID cost and official timing

Here is the fee schedule published by the ICP for a foreign resident, as consulted in November 2026:

| Service | ICP fee | Note |
|---|---|---|
| New card or renewal | 100 AED per year of validity | Validity follows that of the visa |
| Service fee (Smart Service) | 100 AED | Added to every application |
| Urgent service | 150 AED | ICP centre only |
| Replacement of a lost or damaged card | 300 AED | Plus the service fee |
| Data update during a replacement | 150 AED | ICP centre only |
| Late renewal | 20 AED per day | Cap of 1,000 AED |

Examples, ICP fees only:

- 2-year visa: 2 × 100 + 100 = **300 AED**;
- 3-year visa: 3 × 100 + 100 = **400 AED**;
- 5-year visa (Green Visa, retirement visa or 5-year Golden Visa): 5 × 100 + 100 = **600 AED**.

On top of that, depending on the channel, come the typing centre's fees, the cost of the medical test and the visa itself. Searches for "emirates id cost" often return all-inclusive totals that vary from one centre to another: rely on the amount the ICP shows when you pay.

**Timing**: the ICP announces **5 days** of processing. Allow longer if your file is sent back for correction or if the biometrics are delayed.

### Tracking your application ("emirates id status")

According to the ICP FAQ, the status of an application can be tracked on its website with the application number (it starts with 010), in your personal account or through the call centre. One important tip: the ICP regularly warns about **fake websites and fake calls** that imitate its name and logo to demand payments. Only pay and enter your data on icp.gov.ae, in the official app or at an authorised centre.

Searches for "emirates id fine check" often concern fines, whether for a late card or for traffic offences: check them only on official platforms (the ICP for the card, the police and the RTA for traffic).

## The digital Emirates ID and UAE Pass

According to the ICP FAQ, once your card has been issued, its **electronic version** can be viewed in the **UAEICP** app. It can also be used through **UAE Pass**, the national digital identity, which you create by scanning your Emirates ID.

UAE Pass lets you log in to most online public services: GDRFA, ICP, ministries, the Dubai Land Department and many more. Set it up as soon as you have your card.

Still keep the physical card on you: u.ae reminds residents that carrying it is an obligation, and some counterparties may ask to see it.

## Renewing your Emirates ID

### When to renew?

According to u.ae, you can renew **up to six months before expiry** or after it. The ICP specifies that, for a non-citizen, the card must have less than six months of validity left, and the passport must be valid for at least 3 months.

The card expires at the same time as the visa. In practice, the Emirates ID is renewed **together with the visa**, after a new medical test with a "fit" result.

### How to renew?

- on the **UAEICP website or app**, available 24 hours a day;
- at an **ICP service centre** (Khalifa, Al Barsha, Al Rashidiya, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Al Ain, Fujairah, among others);
- through your sponsor or an authorised typing centre, which often files the visa and the card at the same time.

A new photo, different from the one on your current card, is required.

### Late fines

- **Late renewal**: the ICP allows a grace period of 30 days after expiry; beyond that, **20 AED per day**, capped at **1,000 AED** (u.ae and ICP).
- **Unreported change of data**: you must declare a change of personal details (marital status, passport, address, phone) within **30 days**; otherwise the same fine applies, 20 AED per day, capped at 1,000 AED.
- **Exemption**: the ICP offers a service to request an exemption from late fees, granted only in the cases provided for by its decisions. If you were abroad or in a particular situation, ask the ICP before paying.

## Lost, stolen or damaged Emirates ID

1. **Report the loss to the ICP within 7 days** (u.ae).
2. **Request a replacement card** online, in the UAEICP app or at an ICP centre.
3. **Pay** 300 AED for the replacement, plus the 100 AED service fee (and 150 AED if you choose the urgent service).
4. **Redo the biometrics** if the ICP asks you to (from age 15).
5. **Receive the new card** by courier, or collect it at a centre for the urgent service.

The new card keeps **the same expiry date** as the one you lost. If you are abroad when you lose it, make the report online and ask the ICP what to do to return.

## What happens to the Emirates ID when you leave?

According to u.ae, the Emirates ID is **cancelled automatically when your residence visa is cancelled**, for example when you leave your job or close your company. The sponsor requests the visa cancellation (with the GDRFA in Dubai, the ICP elsewhere).

The number, however, stays with you. If you ever come back with a new visa, you will find the same Emirates ID number on your new card.

Note that a residence visa is also cancelled if you stay outside the UAE for more than 180 consecutive days, with some exceptions; the card falls with it. Holders of the [Golden Visa in Dubai](/en/golden-visa-dubai/) are not subject to this rule.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Letting the card expire**: bank, lease and subscriptions can be blocked, and the fine starts running after a month.
- **Handing your card to an employer or landlord**: they are not allowed to keep it.
- **Paying on an unofficial website**: only use icp.gov.ae, the UAEICP app or an authorised centre.
- **Forgetting to declare a new passport**: you have 30 days to update your data.
- **Missing the biometrics appointment**: the application can be cancelled and you have to start again.
- **Neglecting the children**: every resident child has their own card, with their own deadlines.

## Emirates ID checklist

1. Passport valid for more than 3 months (ideally much more).
2. Recent photo that meets the standards, different from the one on the old card in case of renewal.
3. Medical test done with a "fit" result.
4. Application filed on the ICP website or app, or through your sponsor.
5. Biometrics appointment kept (from age 15).
6. UAEICP app installed and UAE Pass account created.
7. Reminder in your calendar six months before expiry.
