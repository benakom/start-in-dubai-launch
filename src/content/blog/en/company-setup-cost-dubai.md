## How much does it cost to set up a company in Dubai?

There is no single price. The cost of setting up a company in Dubai depends on the jurisdiction (mainland or free zone), the activity, the office and the number of visas. Three benchmarks from the official price lists, consulted on 27 November 2026:

- **Licence only, no visa**: Meydan Free Zone advertises a licence **from 12,500 AED**. This price covers the licence and the company registration, not the residence visa, the medical exam or the Emirates ID.
- **Premium free zone, first year with a flexi-desk and one visa**: adding up the lines of the public DMCC price list takes you **above 50,000 AED** (the calculation is detailed below).
- **Mainland**: the cost depends mostly on the rent for the premises, because the lease determines the licence and the visa quota. It is quote-only.

The key point: a price that mentions only the licence is incomplete. The useful budget is the **all-inclusive first year**, then the **renewal**.

To choose your structure first, read our comparison of [mainland vs free zone in Dubai](/en/blog/mainland-vs-free-zone-dubai/) and our guide to [company formation in Dubai](/en/company-formation-dubai/).

## The cost items, in one table

| Item | Paid to | Frequency | Public benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Application, registration, articles | Zone authority or DET | Once | DMCC: 1,035 + 9,020 + 2,020 AED |
| Licence | Zone authority or DET | Every year | Meydan: from 12,500 AED; DMCC: 20,285 AED |
| Office | Free zone or landlord | Every year | DMCC: flexi-desk 16,000 to 19,000 AED; mainland: quote |
| Establishment card | Zone authority (immigration) | Every year | DMCC: 1,825 AED, renewal 2,205 AED |
| Each person's visa | Zone authority, immigration | Every 1 or 2 years | DMCC: 2,237 to 2,972.50 AED per employee visa |
| Emirates ID, medical exam | Official services | With each visa | DMCC: Emirates ID processing 351.40 to 451.40 AED; medical exam extra |
| Health insurance | Insurer | Every year | Quote |
| Bank account | Bank | Every month | Wio: 99 or 249 AED a month |
| Accounting, audit | Firm | Every year | Quote |
| Taxes | Federal Tax Authority | Depending on the activity | Registration free; 0% or 9% depending on profit |

The sections below detail each line.

## 1. The licence and registration

### The DMCC example: a complete public price list

DMCC is one of the few Dubai zones to publish a detailed list of its fees. For a trading or services company, outside any package:

| DMCC price list line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Application fee (once) | 1,035 AED |
| Registration (once) | 9,020 AED |
| Articles of Association (once) | 2,020 AED |
| Licence (first year) | 20,285 AED |
| Establishment card (first year) | 1,825 AED |
| **Total setup and first year** | **34,185 AED** |

DMCC specifies that each fee carries a 20 AED "Knowledge and Innovation Dirhams" surcharge, and that some services are charged excluding VAT. DMCC also offers packages: for example **Jump Start at 43,780 AED** with a flexi-desk (49,941 AED in coworking), presented with one residence visa and up to 3 visas, or **Basic Biz at 35,484 AED**, reserved for individual shareholders. Have it spelled out in writing what each package really includes (immigration fees, establishment card, duration).

### Meydan: the clearest headline price

Meydan Free Zone advertises **12,500 AED** for the licence and company registration, with up to three activity groups included. Its calculator shows **1,000 AED per additional activity** beyond those three groups. The visa, medical exam, Emirates ID, office upgrade and later amendments are charged separately.

### The other free zones

IFZA, Dubai South, JAFZA and DIFC each have their own rates, often given on quote or according to the package. Their characteristics are presented in our guide to the [free zone in Dubai](/en/free-zone-dubai/). Be wary of unofficial sites that display "prices" for these zones: only the zone itself and a written quote are authoritative.

### The mainland

On the mainland, the licence is issued by the Department of Economy and Tourism (DET), which publishes the fees for each procedure (initial approval, trade name reservation, licence issue) on its service pages. These administrative fees weigh little against the **rent for the premises**, generally required with a lease registered on Ejari, and against any approvals from other authorities for regulated activities. The total cost is therefore quote-only, address included.

### The freelancer case

If you work alone, with no employees, a freelance permit often costs less than a company: in the range of 9,000 to 15,000 AED according to the offers published by the authorities (DMCC FreelanceUAE, GoFreelance), excluding insurance. The conditions are detailed in our guide to [freelancing in Dubai](/en/freelance-visa-dubai/).

## 2. The office

In a free zone, a **flexi-desk** (shared workstation) is often enough to start. At DMCC, the price list shows **16,000 to 19,000 AED**, payable in one go at the start of the lease, with administrative fees, deposit and rent included. A private office costs more, but it increases the number of visas you can get.

On the mainland, premises are generally mandatory and are the **first cost item**. The price depends on the neighbourhood, the size and the activity.

## 3. The establishment card

The establishment card registers the company with the immigration services. Without it, no visa. At DMCC, it costs **1,825 AED the first year** and **2,205 AED at renewal**.

## 4. Visas

Each residence visa (yours, your employees' and then your family's) has its own cost. The DMCC price list gives a benchmark for an employee visa applied for from abroad: **2,237 AED for 1 year** and **2,972.50 AED for 2 years**. From inside the UAE, count 2,672 to 4,220 AED depending on the duration and the change of status. On top of that come:

- the processing of the **Emirates ID** (351.40 AED for 1 year, 451.40 AED for 2 years at DMCC);
- the **medical exam**, charged separately;
- **health insurance**, which is mandatory: in Dubai, the employer must insure each employee.

The full process is explained in our guide to the [residence visa in Dubai](/en/residence-visa-dubai/).

## 5. The bank

A business account is not free. As an example, Wio, a bank licensed in the UAE, charges **99 AED a month** for its business account (Essential plan) or **249 AED a month** (Grow plan), whatever the balance. Traditional banks often ask for a minimum average balance and charge fees if you fall below it: check each bank's price list. Everything is explained in our guide to [opening a bank account in Dubai](/en/open-bank-account-dubai/).

## 6. Accounting and audit

Bookkeeping is mandatory for all companies, and an audit is required in two cases under Ministerial Decision No. 84 of 2025:

- a company whose revenue exceeds **50 million AED**;
- any **Qualifying Free Zone Person**, meaning a free zone company that wants the 0% rate, **whatever its revenue**.

These fees are quote-only. For a small free zone company, the annual audit is a cost that many discover too late.

## 7. Taxes and registrations

- **Corporate tax**: registering with the Federal Tax Authority is **free**, but mandatory, even at 0%. Being late is penalised with a **10,000 AED** fine. The rate is 0% up to 375,000 AED of taxable profit and 9% above, with a specific regime in free zones.
- **VAT**: registration is mandatory above 375,000 AED of taxable sales over 12 months, and possible from 187,500 AED.
- **Tax residency certificate**: useful for applying a tax treaty; its fees are published by the Federal Tax Authority and detailed in our guide to [taxes in Dubai](/en/tax-in-dubai/).

## 8. Renewal: the second-year budget

The application, registration and articles fees are paid only once. What comes back every year:

- the **licence** (20,265 AED for one year at DMCC);
- the **establishment card** (2,205 AED at DMCC);
- the **office** or flexi-desk;
- **visas** when they expire, with the Emirates ID and medical exam;
- **health insurance**, the **bank**, **accounting** and, where applicable, the **audit**.

If your free zone company also works on the mainland thanks to the DET permit, add **5,000 AED per 6-month period**.

## A sample budget, calculated from an official price list

For a solo entrepreneur setting up a services company at DMCC, with a flexi-desk and a 2-year visa, no package:

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Setup and first year (application, registration, articles, licence, establishment card) | 34,185 AED |
| Knowledge and Innovation Dirhams (20 AED on each of the 5 fees) | 100 AED |
| Flexi-desk | 16,000 to 19,000 AED |
| 2-year visa applied for from abroad | 2,972.50 AED |
| Emirates ID processing, 2 years | 451.40 AED |
| **Subtotal** | **about 53,700 to 56,700 AED** |

Excluding the medical exam, health insurance, any VAT, bank, accounting and audit. A package can come out cheaper than this line-by-line sum: compare both. This calculation illustrates a public price list at a given date; it is neither a quote nor a market average.

## How to get a reliable quote

1. **Describe your exact activity** and your clients (abroad, free zones, mainland).
2. **Give the number of visas** planned over two years, family included.
3. **Ask for two columns**: first year and renewal.
4. **Demand the list of exclusions**: medical exam, Emirates ID, insurance, VAT, additional activities, audit.
5. **Compare at least two jurisdictions**, including a mainland option if your clients are local.
6. **Check each amount** against the zone's official price list when one exists.
