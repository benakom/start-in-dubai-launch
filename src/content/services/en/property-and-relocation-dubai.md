## Who this support is for

A property purchase in Dubai almost never comes alone. It comes with a visa, a bank account, sometimes a company, often a move. Our support is for:

- **future residents** who want to buy the home where they will live with their family;
- **investors** aiming for a long-term residence, such as the Golden Visa, in addition to their purchase;
- **owners who stay abroad** and want a single contact point in Dubai;
- **entrepreneurs** setting up their company and buying at the same time.

You can live in Morocco, Europe, Canada, Africa or already in the UAE: the coordination is the same.

## Who does what

### Cevitas Real Estate: purchase and management of the property

Choosing the property, negotiation, the transaction and management after purchase are a profession of their own, regulated in Dubai by RERA. We entrust them to our partner [Cevitas Real Estate](https://www.cevitas.ae), a RERA-licensed agency (ORN 49294). START IN DUBAI does not advise on the choice of properties. For renting out a property without being on site, Cevitas presents its service on its page on [rental management in Dubai](https://www.cevitas.ae/gestion-locative-dubai).

### START IN DUBAI: everything around it

We take charge of what depends on your purchase or conditions it:

- **residence**: standard residence visa or Golden Visa, depending on your profile. The conditions are in our [Golden Visa guide for Dubai](/en/golden-visa-dubai/);
- **banking**: personal account, and company account if you buy through a structure. Our guide to [opening a bank account in Dubai](/en/open-bank-account-dubai/) explains the right order of steps;
- **the company**, if your project justifies it, with our [company setup](/en/services/company-setup-dubai/) service;
- **relocation**: the move, the children’s school, health insurance, the first days.

## How the support works

It all starts with a free first conversation. You explain your goal: living there, renting out, obtaining a residence, or several at once. We tell you what each implies, then we put you in touch with Cevitas Real Estate for the property side.

While Cevitas moves forward on the purchase, we prepare in parallel what depends on it. The order matters: for example, without a visa or Emirates ID, opening a resident bank account is generally impossible, and this account is useful for paying service charges and receiving rents. Coordinating these steps avoids waiting for one to start another.

### If you buy in order to settle

We chain with what follows: registration and utility connections, the children’s school, health insurance, driving licence. For a rented home in the first year, the lease must be registered on Ejari, the official system of the Dubai Land Department. Our guide to [moving to Dubai](/en/moving-to-dubai/) gives the complete order of steps, and our [family relocation](/en/services/family-relocation-dubai/) service takes them on.

### If you stay abroad

Cevitas Real Estate handles the management of your property. We remain your contact for the related administrative procedures: bank, possible visa, documents. Also think about your taxes: if you remain tax resident in your country, it may tax the rents received in Dubai, and accounts in the UAE are covered by the automatic exchange of information (CRS). Our guide to [tax in Dubai](/en/tax-in-dubai/) gives the overview.

## What you need to provide

For the relocation part, you will generally be asked for:

- your valid passport;
- proof of income and of the source of funds, which the bank will ask for;
- for the family, legalised marriage and birth certificates;
- if you buy through a company, its incorporation documents.

The documents specific to the property transaction are requested by Cevitas Real Estate.

## Why one point of contact

The purchase, the visa, the bank and the relocation respond to each other: a delay on one blocks the others. With START IN DUBAI, one person keeps the schedule, works directly with Cevitas Real Estate and keeps you informed, in your language (English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Dutch or Italian). For an overview of the ways to invest in the UAE, beyond real estate, read our guide to [investing in Dubai](/en/invest-in-dubai/).

## The mistakes we help you avoid

- **Buying while counting on a Golden Visa without checking the criteria.** The threshold and the value rules are precise.
- **Signing before thinking about the bank.** Payments, service charges and rents need an account, which takes longer to get without residence.
- **Buying through a poorly suited company.** Not all structures can hold a property under the same conditions.
- **Forgetting the taxes of your country of residence** on rents and on the property.
- **Managing each provider separately**, with documents and schedules that contradict each other.
