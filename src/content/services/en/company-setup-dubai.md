## Who this support is for

You want to set up a company in Dubai, but you do not yet know which structure to choose or where to start. Our support is for:

- **entrepreneurs** launching a new activity (consulting, trading, services, digital, import-export);
- **business owners** opening a subsidiary or a structure in the UAE to serve the Gulf, Africa or Asia;
- **freelancers** who hesitate between a freelance licence and a small company;
- **investors** who need a company to hold shares or to settle with a visa.

You can be in Morocco, France, Belgium, Canada, West Africa or already in Dubai: the process is the same, and we adapt it to your situation.

## What our company setup support includes

### The right structure, first of all

The choice between mainland, free zone, offshore and freelance licence determines everything else: the clients you can serve, the number of visas, access to a bank account and your taxes. For example, an offshore company gives no residence visa, and a free zone company has precise rules for selling on the local market. We explain these differences with your real activity, not in theory. To understand the basics before we talk, read our [guide to company formation in Dubai](/en/company-formation-dubai/) and our comparison of [Dubai free zones](/en/free-zone-dubai/).

### The licence, from application to issue

We prepare your file (activities, trade name, partners, manager), submit it to the competent authority and follow every exchange until the licence is issued. If your licence requires premises, we guide you to the right solution: a flexi-desk in a free zone or a registered Ejari lease on the mainland.

### Visas and what follows

Once the licence is issued, we arrange your residence visa, then those of your employees and your family if you wish. The visa and the Emirates ID are often essential to open a bank account, which is why we chain these steps in that order.

### The bank account and the first obligations

The company account is often the longest step. We prepare a consistent file: activity explained, source of funds justified, documents up to date. We also remind you of the tax deadlines: since 1 March 2024, a company must in principle register for corporate tax within 3 months of its creation, failing which a fine of AED 10,000 applies according to the Federal Tax Authority. The details are in our guide to [tax in Dubai](/en/tax-in-dubai/).

## How the support works

It all starts with a free first conversation, by video or phone. We ask precise questions: what do you sell, to whom, from where, how many people will need a visa, what is your timeline. At the end of this conversation, you know which options are realistic for you.

If you decide to go ahead with us, we set a plan with the steps, the documents expected and the moments when your presence in Dubai will be needed. You always know where your file stands and what comes next.

## What you need to provide

The exact list depends on the jurisdiction, but you will generally be asked for:

- a copy of your valid passport (and that of each partner);
- an ID photo in the required format;
- a clear description of the planned activity;
- proof of address in your home country;
- for a company that owns another one, the parent company's documents, sometimes legalised;
- information on the source of funds, which the bank will ask for anyway.

We send you the list adapted to your file and check every document before submission.

## Why one point of contact

Setting up a company in Dubai is not one procedure but a chain: licence, premises, visa, Emirates ID, bank, taxes, then often family, housing and school. When each link is handled by a different provider, files contradict each other and delays grow. With Start in Dubai, one person follows everything, in your language (English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Dutch or Italian). We can also handle [the opening of your bank accounts](/en/services/bank-account-opening-dubai/) and your family's relocation.

## The mistakes we help you avoid

- **Choosing an offshore company in order to settle.** It gives no visa: if you want to live in Dubai, it is not the right structure. Our [offshore company guide](/en/offshore-company-dubai/) details its real uses.
- **Choosing the cheapest free zone without looking at its clients.** A free zone company does not sell freely on the local market.
- **Taking a licence with too few visas.** Adding visas later may force you to change package or premises.
- **Getting the licence without thinking about the bank.** A poorly described activity or poorly justified funds are the first causes of refusal.
- **Forgetting the tax registration.** Even a company with no profit must register on time.
- **Going freelance alone when a company would suit better**, or the reverse: our [freelance in Dubai](/en/freelance-visa-dubai/) guide helps you compare.
