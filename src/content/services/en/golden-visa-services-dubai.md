## Who this support is for

You want to live in Dubai, or simply hold a stable residence there, and you want to be sure to choose the right route. Our visa support is for:

- **investors** aiming for the Golden Visa through a real estate investment, a company or invested capital;
- **entrepreneurs** setting up their company who need a partner or investor visa;
- **skilled employees** who think they meet the conditions of the Golden Visa or the Green Visa;
- **families** who want to bring a spouse and children;
- **residents** who need to renew a visa or switch from one visa to another.

We work with people of all nationalities. Entry rules and document legalisation requirements vary from one country to another, and we take them into account from the start.

## What our Golden Visa and visa support includes

### Knowing what you are entitled to

The Golden Visa is a 5 or 10-year residence depending on the category, renewable, with no sponsor. It is open in particular to real estate investors whose properties total at least AED 2 million, to entrepreneurs, to talents and to highly skilled employees (for example with a monthly salary of at least AED 30,000 and a MOHRE level 1 or 2 position). The detailed conditions are in our [Golden Visa guide for Dubai](/en/golden-visa-dubai/).

If you are not eligible, it is not a dead end: a visa through your company, a freelance licence, a job or the 5-year Green Visa also allow you to reside. Our guide to [residence visas in Dubai](/en/residence-visa-dubai/) compares them. We tell you frankly which route holds up for you, before you commit to any fees.

### A complete file from the first filing

Most delays come from missing or poorly legalised documents. The UAE does not accept the apostille: marriage and birth certificates must go through consular legalisation. We give you the exact list and the order of steps, and we check every document before filing.

### Follow-up through to the Emirates ID

We follow your application with the GDRFA in Dubai, or the ICP depending on the case, then we organise the medical test and fingerprinting with you. The Emirates ID is your resident card: without it, no Ejari lease and, in most cases, no resident bank account.

### Your family's visas

A resident can sponsor a spouse and children if they earn at least AED 4,000 per month, or AED 3,000 with accommodation provided, according to u.ae. Female residents can also sponsor their husband and children. We prepare these files in parallel with yours so the family can follow quickly. For the rest of the move (housing, schools, health), see our [family relocation to Dubai](/en/services/family-relocation-dubai/) service.

## How the support works

The first conversation is free, by video or phone. We review your profile, your income, your project and your family. You leave with a clear answer: which routes are possible, which are not, and what you would need to gather.

If you go ahead with us, we set a schedule: documents to gather in your country, legalisations, date of your trip to Dubai, filing, then family visas.

## What you need to provide

Depending on the category, you will notably be asked for:

- your valid passport and an ID photo;
- the supporting documents specific to the category: title deed, company licence, employment contract and salary certificate, diplomas;
- legalised marriage and birth certificates for the family;
- health insurance valid in the UAE.

With START IN DUBAI, one person follows your residence and everything that follows from it, in your language (English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Dutch or Italian). ## The mistakes we help you avoid

- **Filing a Golden Visa without checking the category.** A file outside the criteria loses weeks and fees.
- **Arriving with documents that are only apostilled.** They will be refused: consular legalisation is required.
- **Forgetting the 180-day rule.** A standard visa is in principle cancelled after more than 180 consecutive days outside the country, which does not apply to the Golden Visa or the Green Visa.
- **Signing a lease before having your Emirates ID.** The Ejari and utility connections become complicated.
- **Letting the family's health insurance lapse**, which is mandatory for every sponsored person.
- **Confusing the Golden Visa with citizenship.** It is a residence permit, not a passport: our guide to [investing in Dubai](/en/invest-in-dubai/) puts each route in its place.
