Frequently asked questions

Your questions, our answers

Does my request commit me to anything?

No. You tell us who you are and what you want to do in Dubai, and an advisor gets back to you. Then you decide, freely, whether you want our support.

What happens after my request?

An advisor messages you on WhatsApp or calls you for a first conversation about your project: your goals, your timeline and the steps to plan.

Do I need an Emirati partner to set up my company?

No, in most cases. In a free zone, a company can be 100% foreign-owned, and since 2021 this has also been possible on the mainland for most activities. A few regulated activities remain subject to specific conditions.

What about taxes?

There is no personal income tax. Companies in principle pay 9% corporate tax on the portion of profit above AED 375,000, with a 0% rate possible for some free zone companies on their qualifying income, and 5% VAT. Your situation in your home country matters too: we discuss it from the first conversation.

How long does it take to get set up?

It depends on the project. A free zone company can be set up in a few days or a few weeks once the file is complete; the bank account and visas often take several more weeks. For a family, it's best to start 4 to 6 months before your arrival date, especially for housing and visas.

Do you also handle real estate and the management of my property?

Yes: search, purchase, then letting and management of your property, with the Cevitas Real Estate team, a RERA-licensed agency (ORN 49294). And if you move here, the same team takes care of the rest: company, visas, family, university studies.

Do you also help with university studies?

Yes. For a student who wants to study in Dubai or elsewhere in the UAE: choosing the programme and the university, the application, student visa, housing and settling in, with their parents at every step.

What language is the support in?

Yours: Arabic, French, English, Spanish, Dutch or Italian.
